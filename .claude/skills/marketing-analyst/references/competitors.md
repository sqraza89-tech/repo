# Competitor & market research

## Pick competitors
Use the list in the brand profile. If it's `TODO`: search the ICP's key buying queries, note who
ranks and who AI engines cite, propose 3–5 (mix of local and global), and ask the user to confirm
before going deep. Save the confirmed list back to the brand profile.

## For each competitor (public pages only)
| Area | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Positioning | homepage H1 + subhead, who they say they serve |
| Offers | service lines, packaged offers (assessments, audits, fixed-scope pilots), pricing signals |
| Proof | named clients, case studies, certifications, partner badges |
| Conversion | primary CTA, lead magnets, booking vs form |
| Content | blog cadence, topics, formats, FAQ/answer-first structure, gated assets |
| Search/AEO | which of our target queries they rank or get cited for |
| LinkedIn | followers, post cadence, formats, typical engagement, employee advocacy |

## Market signals
- Regulatory or industry changes that create a buying trigger for an ICP (e.g. new regulations, deadlines)
- Rising queries / topics in the ICP's space (Google Trends, search suggestions, industry news)
- Cite every external source with a link and date

## Synthesis
1. **Table stakes** — what everyone says (don't lead with it)
2. **White space** — what buyers ask that nobody answers well
3. **Our credible edge** — what we can say with approved proof that they can't
4. **Threats** — where a competitor clearly out-positions us for an ICP
Turn each into an action (content, page, offer, proof) with an owner.
