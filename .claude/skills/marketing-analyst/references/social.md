# Social checklist (LinkedIn first)

## Company page (admin analytics, last 90 days)
- Followers: total, growth, and **who** — job function, seniority, industry, location vs the ICPs
- Posts: impressions, engagement rate, clicks, reposts — top 5 and bottom 5, and what they have in common (format, topic, ICP, time)
- Visitors: page views by function/seniority
- Cadence vs the content calendar: planned vs actually posted

## Ambassadors
- Did each post what was planned? Reach of ambassador posts vs company posts
- Profile hygiene: title matches the brand profile, headline mentions the company, featured section links to a service page

## Content fit
- Share of posts per ICP and per funnel stage vs the plan
- Posts that drove site clicks (UTM or LinkedIn click data) → which topics convert, not just engage
- Comments from ICP-looking people (title/company) — the best signal of real interest

## Competitors on LinkedIn
- Their cadence, formats, topics, engagement per post, employee advocacy — see `competitors.md`

## Output
Top formats/topics to do more of, what to stop, 3 experiments for next month (one variable each, with a metric).
