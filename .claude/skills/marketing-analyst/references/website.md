# Website & conversion checklist

Check the homepage, each service/pillar page, the top 5 landing pages by traffic, and contact/forms.

## Message
- [ ] Above the fold: who it's for, what problem, what to do next — in the ICP's words
- [ ] H1 is keyword-first and plain
- [ ] One primary CTA per page, matched to the page's funnel stage
- [ ] Proof near the CTA (case, logo, credential, number from approved claims)

## Conversion path
- [ ] Every CTA works and goes to a live, relevant destination (click through)
- [ ] Forms: number of fields, a "what problem are you solving?" field, confirmation message, privacy notice
- [ ] Contact options match what sales can actually answer (phone/email/booking)
- [ ] Next-step links from blogs to service pages exist

## Trust & consistency
- [ ] Company facts identical across About, footer, service pages, LinkedIn
- [ ] Privacy policy / cookie consent present and current
- [ ] No placeholder text, broken images, dead links, dated "2024" copy

## Technical (spot-check; use PageSpeed Insights via browser for key pages)
- [ ] Unique title + meta per page; single H1; canonical present
- [ ] Mobile layout OK; LCP/CLS reasonable
- [ ] Doubled brand suffix or other template bugs in titles

## Analytics (GA4)
- Sessions by landing page, segmented: buyer pages vs careers/other
- Engagement rate and average engagement time on service pages
- Conversions/key events: form submits, CTA clicks — are they even tracked? If not, that's action #1
- Referral from AI engines (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, copilot, gemini) — track as its own channel
