## Decisions taken in the draft

- **Address = Karachi, Country = Pakistan** (not the San Mateo HQ). Reason: Huzaifa's contact number
  is +92, so a US address invites an obvious question; and the address decides which Pipedrive
  partner manager picks it up. Pakistan routes to a thin-coverage market where xLoop is strong;
  the US is the most saturated Pipedrive partner market and xLoop has no local sales presence.
- **Business type:** Consultation, Implementation, Integrations, Custom Development, Resell
  Subscriptions. Agency / SEO / Social / Demand Generation deliberately excluded — they route to
  Pipedrive's marketing-agency partner track.
- **Countries of operation:** Pakistan, UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, USA — five that each have a real
  project or registered entity, which also sidesteps the unresolved C3 country-count conflict.
- **Industries:** Financial or Credit Services, Energy, Health, Retail, Government/public services,
  IT Services, Consulting (Priority 1–3 per approved claim B3, plus own sector).
- **Company sizes targeted:** 10-49, 50-250, 250+.
- **First-year seats: 31 – 50.** Realistic, not inflated — Pipedrive tiers partner status on this
  number and holds partners to it.

## Promotion recommendation: not yet, and small when it happens

Position given to Sana: **do not announce.** It is an application, not a partnership — nothing
exists until Pipedrive approves, an agreement is signed and xLoop appears in the partner directory.

Once approved, in priority order:

1. **Optimise the Pipedrive partner directory listing, not the website.** That directory is where
   SMB CRM buyers actually search. Highest return by a distance, costs nothing.
2. Add the Pipedrive logo to the partners strip beside AWS, Microsoft, Snowflake, Databricks,
   Salesforce. Free credibility, no dilution, no new page.
3. One LinkedIn post, framed as capability not status.
4. **No landing page yet** — the site still has 100% duplicate titles/metas and missing canonicals
   with zero non-brand organic clicks, so a new page earns nothing until engineering fixes that.

**Why the restraint:** Pipedrive is SMB CRM at ~$30–60/seat/month. The recommended architecture is
*Make AI work / Make AI safe / Make AI possible*, sold to CIO/CTO/CISO. A reseller badge sits
outside all three pillars and pulls toward the "IT reseller" framing the terminology guidance
already flags as a risk. It means nothing to the 87 NEPRA CEOs in
[[2026-08-25-nepra-followup-letters-project]].

## The strategic question underneath the form

Ticking "Resell Subscriptions" and committing to 31–50 seats commits xLoop to an **SMB motion** —
different buyer, price point, sales cycle, and possibly Tier 1 support obligations. This appears
nowhere in the Marketing Brain or in [[2026-09-07-xloop-bucket-1-region-targeting]], whose whole
thesis is enterprise managed delivery in Tier 1 European and Gulf markets.

It is a reasonable adjacent revenue line and a door-opener into accounts that later buy data and AI
work — but it is a second business, not an extension of the first.
