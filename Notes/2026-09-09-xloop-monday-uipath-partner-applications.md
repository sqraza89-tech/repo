## Strategic position given to Sana

**These are not three versions of the same bet.**

- **UiPath is the only one that fits xLoop's actual positioning.** Enterprise automation sold to
  banks, government, energy and manufacturing — the Priority 1–3 industries and the existing
  CIO/CTO/CISO buyer. Maps onto the *Make AI work / Make AI possible* pillars, and UiPath's
  agentic-AI pivot sits on top of xLoop's story. Push this hardest, and start now because it is the
  only one needing upfront investment (certifications).
- **Pipedrive and monday.com are the same bet twice** — same SMB/mid-market buyer, same low-ticket
  motion, and monday.com CRM competes head-on with Pipedrive. Neither vendor disqualifies you for
  the other; the risk is internal: first-year seat commitments to two competing products with no
  CRM resale history and nobody dedicated to either.
- **If picking one of the two: monday.com.** Bigger TAM, better partner economics, and decisively —
  it is not a CRM, so it does not collide with clients who already have one. Work-management is a
  wedge into almost any mid-market account, and the app marketplace gives xLab a second revenue
  line Pipedrive has no equivalent for.
- **Submit both, resource one.** Applications are cheap and the qualification calls are free market
  research — but the resourcing choice should be deliberate, not drift.

**Promotion position unchanged:** nothing on website or socials until something is signed, and then
a partner-directory listing plus a logo strip, not a campaign. Three partner badges announced at
once reads as a firm broadening out — the opposite of what the Marketing Brain says xLoop needs.
