personal brand advocates, a plan for how to run it, a topic and content plan for the next quarter,
and a look at UGC/affiliate.

at once, not one.

## How it runs

### Monthly (last week of the month before)

- Sana writes a **1-page cluster brief**:
  - the one line
  - 3 talking points
  - approved proof to use
  - what not to say
- Brief goes to all 5 ambassadors + Omais

### Weekly

- Drafts prepared in each ambassador's voice, based on a 20-minute kickoff interview
- Ambassador edits and approves in ~10 minutes, then posts it themselves
- Company post goes first (Tue). Ambassadors post Tue–Thu, mornings Pakistan / Gulf time
- Every draft is checked against `05_brand/approved_claims.md` before it goes to the ambassador

### Design (Omais)

- Build templates once in the first week of October: quote card, carousel, headshot frame, event card
- Reuse them after that. Needs ~2 days of focused time up front

### Guardrails

| ✅ OK to say | ❌ Never |
|---|---|
| 70+ engineers · founded 2022 · work in Europe (Hungary) | Name MAF, Kayali, SS&C, IRD, ACT Wind, any NEPRA prospect |
| Certified ISO 27001 / 42001 **Lead Auditors** | "xLoop is ISO certified" · "NEPRA-approved" · "Category-1" |
| Microsoft Partner | Solutions Partner · anything about Fabric |
| Anonymized cases at proof-library wording | Unsourced stats (99.7%, 92% retention, 80%, 30%...) |
| Named: Alfalah Investments, Beythak, Eatsy, HBL AM, Serefin, Skillforte, FitLynk, Cloud Titans, logo wall | Paraphrased testimonials in quote marks |
| US spelling, plain words, answer first | Fear-selling, "cutting-edge", "world-class", gimmicky hooks |
