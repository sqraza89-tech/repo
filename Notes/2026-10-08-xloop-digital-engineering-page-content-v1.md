## 11. FAQ · `#faq`

**H2:** Digital Engineering FAQ

*All 8 go into FAQPage schema, with text identical to the page.*

**What is digital engineering?**
Digital engineering is the work of designing, building and running software, using modern practices such as cloud, automation, data and AI. A digital engineering partner provides the engineers and the engineering discipline to deliver your products and platforms.

**How is this different from staff augmentation?**
You're not just getting people. You get engineers matched to your stack, a named xLoop contact, monthly reporting, and access to our architects and AI specialists when the work needs them. If you'd rather hand over a whole outcome, we can run it as a scoped project instead.

**How quickly can engineers start?**
It depends on the roles and skills you need. We share matching profiles first, you interview them, and engineers start once you've chosen. The first call tells you what's realistic for your roles.

**Will I get to interview the engineers?**
Yes. You see profiles and interview engineers before they join, so you choose who works on your product.

**Can we scale the team up or down?**
Yes. Team size follows your roadmap. You can add engineers as work grows, or reduce the team with one month's notice.

**Do your engineers use AI coding tools?**
Yes. Our engineers use AI coding assistants, including Cursor and Claude, to read code, write tests and draft changes faster. Every change is reviewed, tested and owned by an engineer. Whether these tools are used on your codebase is agreed with you before work starts.

**Who owns the code and IP?**
You do. Code written for your product belongs to you, under the terms agreed in your contract.

**How is digital engineering priced?**
On demand engineers are priced per engineer per month, on time and materials or a fixed monthly rate. Scoped projects are priced on the agreed scope. We'll give you a proposal after the first call.
