# Ada Lovelace Day LinkedIn post: women of tech

## What it is

- LinkedIn post highlighting the company's female engineers as women of tech
- Goal: shareability on LinkedIn and curiosity about the brand
- Brand: xLoop (company page)
- Format: PDF carousel, 1080 x 1350 px, up to 5 engineers, one slide each
- Closing: soft brand line, no CTA
