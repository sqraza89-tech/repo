What changed from v1

  • Rounded, bubbly sans → fashion-magazine serifs with lots of white space
  • Safe park greens → richer, bolder colour (cherry, cobalt, fuchsia)
  • Cartoon icons (heart, hills) → refined monograms and simple shapes that work on merch
  • The messaging leans aspirational: "club", "est. 2026", "strong is the new glow"
