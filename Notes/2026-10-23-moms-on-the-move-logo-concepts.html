Moms on the Move: logo concepts

Active moms club · school drop-off walks and jogs, park pilates, monthly brunch · draft v1, 23 Oct 2026

A · The Loop

A walking trail drawn as one open heart, with a coral dot for the mom on the move. The heart is for the moms, the loop is the park route. Friendly, simple, and the icon works on its own as an Instagram avatar.

B · Sunrise Club Badge

A club crest: morning sun over the park hills, with the walking path winding through. It feels like a community you join, and fits T-shirts, water bottles, tote bags and brunch invites.

C · Stride Wordmark

The sportiest option. A handwritten "moms on the" for warmth, then a bold leaning MOVE with speed lines. The O is a running track with the mom dot on it. Best if you want it to read "fitness" first.

How they look as a profile picture

Instagram and WhatsApp group icons are tiny, so the mark has to hold up at this size.

Suggested palette: "Morning in the Park"

Fresh and energetic without going baby-pink. Green says outdoors and health, coral brings the workout energy, butter yellow is the morning sun, and blush plus cream keep it soft and feminine.

Fonts

  • Outfit (free, Google Fonts): bold and rounded, for the name and headlines
  • Caveat Brush (free, Google Fonts): handwritten accent for warm touches like "see you at the park!"

Usage tips

  • Use roughly 60% cream/blush backgrounds, 30% pine, 10% coral and butter as accents
  • Don't use coral or butter for small text on cream; it's hard to read. Use pine or ink
  • Colour-code activities if you like: walks and jogs = pine, park pilates = sage, monthly brunch = blush + coral
