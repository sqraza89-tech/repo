# Moms on the Move — ChatGPT logo prompts (v3 direction)

Related: [[2026-10-23-moms-on-the-move-logo-concepts-v2]], brand profile `Reference/brands/momsonthemove.md`

## Direction (from Sana's references, 23 Oct 2026)
- **References:** an orange abstract runner made of ribbon shapes with a circle head; a white brush-stroke figure leaping; lululemon "GET INTO IT" ads; a "Move" campaign with orange/fuchsia motion blur and a body-positive model
- **Feel:** cool, confident, chic. Comfortable in your body *and* getting into shape. Aspirational, never mumsy
- **Colours:** electric orange + hot fuchsia (gradient OK), deep plum for contrast, warm cream background
- **Logo style:** abstract human figure from one or two flowing strokes, plus a heavy, wide, geometric sans wordmark

## How to use
1. Paste the **master prompt** first. It sets the brief
2. Then paste the option prompts one at a time (GPT does better with one concept per image)
3. Iterate on the one you like: "same mark, thicker strokes", "make the head circle smaller", "show it as a profile picture"
4. Final logo gets redrawn as clean vector (SVG) before use; GPT images are raster and not perfectly symmetrical

## Master prompt
```
You are a senior brand designer at a top studio. Design a logo for "Moms on the Move", a premium active moms club. After school drop-off, mothers (ages 26–40) meet in a park nearby to walk and jog together, do mat pilates outdoors, and have a monthly brunch.

Brand feeling: cool, confident, chic and energetic, like a premium athleisure brand (think the attitude of modern activewear campaigns), not a community-centre flyer. It celebrates feeling good in your body AND getting stronger. Body-positive, joyful, aspirational: something a mom would proudly wear on a cap, tee or water bottle.

Visual style:
- An abstract human figure in motion, built from one or two bold flowing ribbon or brush strokes with a simple circle for the head. Dynamic, mid-stride or arms-up joyful, slightly leaning forward
- Minimal, geometric, confident. Must read clearly at tiny sizes (Instagram profile picture)
- Wordmark in a heavy, wide, modern geometric sans-serif, tight letter spacing, all lowercase or all caps
- Colours: electric orange #FF4D12 and hot fuchsia #E6198A (a smooth orange-to-fuchsia gradient is welcome), deep plum #3B0A2E for contrast, warm cream #FFF4EA background

Avoid: strollers, babies, hearts, ponytail clip-art, dumbbells, stick figures, generic swooshes, anything that looks like an existing sports brand's logo, retro or cartoon styles, thin script fonts, pastel or "mumsy" looks, 3D effects, mockups.

Output: flat 2D vector-style logo, centred on a plain cream background, crisp edges, no shadows. Spell the name exactly: "moms on the move".
```

## Option prompts (paste after the master prompt)

### 1 · Ribbon Stride
```
Option 1 "Ribbon Stride": a single continuous ribbon that folds into a woman mid-stride, one arm reaching forward, with a circle head. The ribbon flows from electric orange into hot fuchsia. Place the wordmark "moms on the move" to the right in a heavy wide sans, plum colour. Show the full logo plus the symbol alone.
```

### 2 · Together
```
Option 2 "Together": two abstract figures striding side by side, built from overlapping ribbon strokes so their shapes interlock, one orange, one fuchsia, the overlap in plum. It should feel like friends moving together, not a crowd. Wordmark "moms on the move" stacked below, centred.
```

### 3 · Arms Up
```
Option 3 "Arms Up": one bold, expressive brush-stroke figure with arms raised in a joyful, free, comfortable-in-my-body pose, mid-step. The energy of dancing in the morning sun. Solid fuchsia figure with an orange circle head. Wordmark "moms on the move" below in plum.
```

### 4 · The M in Motion
```
Option 4 "M in Motion": a lettermark where a bold lowercase or uppercase "M" is also a figure in stride. The M's strokes become legs and an arm, and a circle head sits above. Orange-to-fuchsia gradient fill. Must work alone as an app or profile icon. Show it with the wordmark "moms on the move" beside it.
```

### 5 · Wordmark-first
```
Option 5 "Wordmark": a bold typographic logo. "MOVE" is huge in a heavy, wide geometric sans, with "moms on the" small and tracked out above it. Add one motion detail: the O becomes a circle head with a short ribbon swoosh trailing like a running figure, or the letters have a sliced speed-cut. White type on an orange-to-fuchsia motion-blur gradient background, plus a version in plum on cream.
```

## Follow-up prompts that help
- `Show the chosen logo as: an Instagram profile circle, a cap embroidery, a tote bag, and a water bottle. Flat mockups, same colours.`
- `Give me 3 variations of option X: one bolder, one more minimal, one more feminine. Keep everything else the same.`
- `Same logo in one colour only (plum on cream, and cream on fuchsia).`

## Fonts to match in the final build (free, Google Fonts)
- Archivo (Expanded / ExtraBold) or Unbounded for the wordmark, the closest feel to the lululemon-style heavy wide type
- Jost or Inter for small text
