Four original marks in the style of your references: thin even lines, arcs, a circle head and lots of open space. None of them copies a reference logo. Each is shown as the full logo, reversed out, and as a profile picture.

1 · Arc Stride sleek · athletic

Just two crossing arcs and a head: a woman mid-stride. The most minimal and "luxury sportswear" of the four.

2 · Rising M lettermark · momentum

A lowercase "m" whose humps get taller, then leaps up into an arm with a head. It's the M for moms and the feeling of getting stronger each week. This is the strongest option for a profile picture.

3 · Together community · club

Three moms, arms linked: one flowing line makes the shoulders and arms. It says "club" and "you won't do this alone", the warmest of the four.

4 · Free Spirit feminine · body-confident

A curvy line-drawn woman with one arm up: waist, hip and a bent knee. It's about feeling good in your body, and it suits pilates and stretch days best.
