# Moms on the Move: final brand decision

## Decision
- **Logo:** The Loop (open-heart walking trail + dot)
- **Colours:** heart line Purple `#9D8FE0`, dot + "ON THE MOVE" Coral `#F5967A`, "moms" Ink `#2B2540`; supporting Rose `#E0739A`, background Cream `#FFF9F5`
- **Why purple + coral, not sage:** the client felt sage + peach looked like the Pampers brand
- **Typography:** Outfit only (ExtraBold 800 headlines, SemiBold 600 tagline/subheads, Regular 400 body)
