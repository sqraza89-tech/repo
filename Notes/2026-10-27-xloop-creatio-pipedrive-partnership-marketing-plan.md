# xLoop: Creatio + Pipedrive partnership marketing plan

Requested by **Huzaifa Valika, VP Enterprise Sales**. Builds on
[[2026-09-08-xloop-pipedrive-solution-provider-application]] and
[[2026-10-19-xloop-partner-form-answers]].

## The short version

- **Don't sell the badge. Sell the outcome.** Buyers don't care that we're a partner. They care that
  someone can get a CRM live, move their data into it cleanly, connect it to their other systems and
  put AI on top. That is work xLoop already does (data migration, integration, AI agents)
- **Two partners, two buyers. Keep the stories separate:**
  - **Creatio** (no-code CRM + workflow, agentic AI, strong in banking and financial services) →
    mid-market and enterprise, regulated industries. Fits our ICPs and the "Make AI work" pillar
  - **Pipedrive** (simple sales CRM) → growth-stage and SMB sales teams. A new, faster, lower-ticket
    motion. A door-opener, not our core story
- **Where the money is:** partner directories, the vendors' own co-marketing, and direct outreach by
  sales. Not paid ads (no budget) and not a big website launch
- **Timeline:** 1 week of setup, 2-week launch, then a 6-week demand push (Mon 2 Nov 2026 to Fri 18 Dec 2026)

## Positioning

**One line (internal):** xLoop implements Creatio and Pipedrive, migrates your data into them,
connects them to the rest of your stack, and adds AI that actually gets used.

| | Creatio | Pipedrive |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | CIO, CTO, Head of Digital, Head of Operations at banks, insurers, energy, public sector | Founder, Head of Sales, Sales Ops at growth-stage firms |
| Their problem (their words) | "Our CRM is old, slow to change and IT is the bottleneck" · "We want AI in our workflows, not another pilot" | "Our pipeline lives in spreadsheets" · "We bought a CRM and nobody uses it" |
| What we lead with | Implementation + migration from legacy CRM + integration with core systems + AI agents in workflows | Fast setup, migration from spreadsheets or old CRM, integrations, training so the team adopts it |
| Our proof | Data migration, integration and AI agent delivery (approved claims only). **No Creatio project yet** | Same. **No Pipedrive project yet** |
| Sales cycle | Months, multi-stakeholder | Weeks |
| ICP fit | ICP1 (AI Transformation Sponsor), ICP2 (Data Modernization Owner) | Outside the current ICP set. Treat as a new segment |

**Words to avoid:** "reseller", "official partner of" as a headline, "certified experts" (until
people are actually certified), any tier we don't hold yet.

## Phase 1: Launch (Mon 2 Nov to Fri 13 Nov 2026)

### 1. Partner directories (highest return, free)

- Complete our profile in the **Creatio partner catalog** and the **Pipedrive partner directory**:
  services, industries, regions (Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, USA, Europe), languages, contact
- Buyers searching for an implementer go here first, and the vendors route leads from here
- Owner: Sana writes, Huzaifa submits. Effort: 1 day

### 2. Vendor co-marketing (ask for it early)

- Pipedrive offers **marketing development funds (MDF)** and channel marketing support. Creatio
  runs co-marketing with partners. Ask both partner managers in the first call:
  - Will they share our announcement on their channels?
  - What MDF or co-marketing budget can we claim, and against what?
  - Can we get **partner-referred leads** in our regions (Pakistan and the GCC are thinly covered)?
  - Do they have ready-made campaign kits (emails, one-pagers, demo scripts) we can co-brand?
- Owner: Huzaifa

### 3. Website (small, fast)

- Add Creatio and Pipedrive logos to the **partners strip** (beside AWS, Microsoft, Snowflake,
  Databricks, Salesforce)
- **One page: "CRM Implementation and Integration"** under the Digital Transformation cluster, with
  a Creatio section and a Pipedrive section. One page, not two, until there is demand to justify more
  - Built for searches like "Creatio implementation partner UAE" and "Pipedrive partner Pakistan"
    (check volumes first; these are small but high-intent)
  - Value CTA, not "Talk to us". Example: **"Get your CRM migration plan"** (only if delivery agrees
    to produce one in a first call)
- Every social and email CTA points here, never the homepage
- Owner: Sana (copy), Omais/Rafay (design), production devs (build). Effort: about 2 weeks end to end

### 4. Announcement on LinkedIn

- **Company page:** one carousel. Slide 1 is the buyer's problem, not "We're proud to announce".
  Then what we now do with Creatio and Pipedrive, then one CTA to the new page
- **Huzaifa's own post** (personal angle: why we chose these two platforms and who they're for)
- **CEO and COO reshare** with one line each
- Tag both vendors so they can reshare
- Owner: Sana (copy), Omais (carousel). Needs 2 working days' notice

2. **Banks and financial services in Pakistan and the GCC** for Creatio (Creatio's strongest vertical, and ours)
3. **Growth-stage companies in Pakistan and the UAE** for Pipedrive
4. **Partner-referred leads** from both vendors' channel teams

## Risks

- **No CRM delivery proof yet.** Until the first project ships, we sell on adjacent proof (data
  migration, integration, AI agents). The first Creatio and first Pipedrive projects should become
  anonymous case studies as fast as possible
- **Pipedrive pulls toward SMB.** Different buyer, price point and support load. Keep it off the
  homepage and out of the enterprise story
- **Site defects** (doubled brand suffix in titles) will hold back the new page in search. Fix before launch
- **Marketing capacity** is Sana 2–3 h/day + Omais. This plan is sized to that; anything extra needs dropping something else
