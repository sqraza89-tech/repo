# xCelerate LinkedIn Brand Awareness Video · v3 (Mari + PPL)

Full scene script built on the outline and decisions in [[2026-10-27-xloop-xcelerate-linkedin-video-script-v2-ppl]]. Earlier Mari-only version: [[2026-10-19-xloop-mari-cohort-linkedin-video-script]]. Facts from [[2026-10-13-xloop-xcelerate-training-pages-content-v1]].

## Brief

- **Goal:** paid LinkedIn brand awareness. Show other companies that xCelerate's sponsored programs end in careers
- **Story:** xLoop's own video. Highlights and impact of two CSR programs. No sponsor quotes, no Naseem Qamar
- **Length:** 47 seconds · 4:5 or 1:1 · burned in text, music bed, optional VO
- **Audience:** HR and L&D heads, CSR and sustainability leaders, foundations, development programs

## Claim rules

- **Mari:** 44 graduates, including 5 women · 5 months · 100% placed into internships and employment
- **PPL:** no graduate count (15 is too small to feature) · no placement percentage · only "Class of 2025", "Graduates certified", "Top performers recruited"
- **"Selected on merit"** sits in the Mari scene only until it's confirmed for PPL
- Both sponsors appear as **CSR funders only**. No energy industry framing
