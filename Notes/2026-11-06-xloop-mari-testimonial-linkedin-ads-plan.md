# Mari Testimonial Video Ads · Paid Social Plan v3

**Official brief (Ali Abbasi, email):** Nasir wants this promoted on social media with a budget of about USD 300 to 500, focused on **Punjab and Federal government entities** such as PITB, ITID, PASHA, PSEB, MoITT, NAVTTC, PMYP, CMITIP, PSDA and PSDF. We choose the main platforms. Execution next week.

**v3 changes (6 Nov):** audience moves from corporate HR / CSR to government and public-sector skills bodies; two platforms (LinkedIn + Facebook); budget USD 450; launch Wed 11 Nov; no lead form or retargeting campaign (budget too small to split, and it suits an awareness goal better).

Related: [[2026-10-19-xloop-mari-cohort-linkedin-video-script]]

## Campaign at a glance

- **What:** the Mari testimonial video (4 graduates), plus short cutdowns made from the same footage
- **Goal:** put the Mari program in front of Punjab and Federal government skills, IT and youth bodies as a proven model they can partner with or fund
- **Main message:** a pioneering program built to end in employment. Collaboration (Mari Energies × xCelerate by xLoop), fostering careers and lives, not just training
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn (main) + Facebook
- **Duration:** 4 weeks, Wed 11 Nov to Tue 8 Dec 2026
- **Budget:** USD 450 (about PKR 126,000)

## Platform choice

| Platform | Budget | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | USD 300 | Only platform that can target people **by employer**: staff at PITB, PSEB, MoITT, NAVTTC, PSDF and the rest |
| **Facebook** (feed + reels) | USD 150 | Where these bodies and officials are most active in Pakistan. Can't target by employer, so it targets Lahore + Islamabad / Rawalpindi by interest. Cheap reach that builds the program's public image |

**Not chosen:**
- **Instagram:** younger, consumer audience. Facebook placements already cover Meta's reach
- **X (Twitter):** government accounts are active there, but access and ad billing in Pakistan are uncertain. Can be added later if the team wants

## Audience

### LinkedIn

- **Companies (by name, where they have a LinkedIn page):** PITB, ITID, PASHA, PSEB, MoITT, NAVTTC, PMYP, CMITIP, PSDA, PSDF
- **OR:** industry "Government Administration" + job functions Program & Project Management, Education, Operations, Business Development, Community & Social Services
- **Seniority:** Manager and above
- **Location:** Punjab + Islamabad Capital Territory
- LinkedIn needs at least 300 matched members to run. If the named list is too small, the industry layer keeps it running

### Facebook

- **Location:** Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi
- **Age:** 25 to 55
- **Interests:** public administration, government, skills development, vocational education, youth development, Pakistan IT industry
- **Exclude:** people who applied through the earlier Mari student campaign, if that audience is still in the ad account

## Frequency and when to change collaterals

### Schedule

- **Ads run every day.** Target: each person sees an ad 2 to 3 times a week
- **Weekly numbers** to Ali every Monday; final report Wed 9 Dec

### When to swap a video (check every Monday)

- **Frequency above 4** (same people seeing it too often): add a new cutdown or widen the audience
- **View rate falls by a third** compared with its first week: rotate in the next cutdown
- **One video clearly behind** the others after 7 days: pause it and move its money to the winner
- Don't change anything in the first 3 to 4 days. The platforms need that time to learn

### Making changes after launch

- New collateral or wording change: Sana (content), then Omais (design), 2 working days' notice
- Changes to the claim, Mari's name or the numbers: training team sign-off
- Budget changes: Ali's OK

## Free support that matters most with this audience

- **Direct sharing:** the training team sends the video by email or LinkedIn message to contacts they already have at PSDF, NAVTTC, PITB, PSEB and the others. With a named list of 10 entities, this reaches decision makers more surely than any ad
- **Don't tag or name the government entities in ads or posts** unless there's an existing relationship. It can read as an endorsement they haven't given

## Message rule (needs Nasir's sign-off)

- Requested line: "pioneering and only program in Pakistan that guarantees employment"
- Platforms reject or can pull ads with claims we can't back up ("only", "first", "guarantees"). With a government audience, an overclaim also damages credibility
- **Option A (as requested):** use it only if the training team can confirm (1) placement was guaranteed in the program terms, and (2) no other program in Pakistan offers this
- **Option B (recommended):** "A pioneering program in Pakistan built to end in employment. Every graduate placed into an internship or job."
