## Campaign at a glance

- **What:** the Mari testimonial video (4 graduates), plus short cutdowns made from the same footage
- **Goal:** show companies that xCelerate's program ends in careers, and bring in conversations for the next sponsored cohort or corporate training
- **Main message:** collaboration (Mari Energies × xCelerate by xLoop) and the employability outcome. Fostering careers and lives, not just training
- **Platform:** LinkedIn
- **Duration:** 4 weeks, Mon 16 Nov to Sun 13 Dec 2026
- **Budget:** USD 476 (about PKR 133,000)

## Why 4 weeks

- LinkedIn needs at least USD 10/day per campaign. With USD 400 to 500, that allows two small campaigns for 4 weeks
- Shorter (2 weeks) leaves no time to learn what works and retarget. Longer (6+ weeks) spreads the money too thin to show up enough
- Ends before the Christmas / year-end slowdown, while companies are planning next year's CSR budgets

## Platforms

| Platform | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn (paid)** | All of the budget | Reaches the decision makers: HR, L&D, CSR heads and CEOs of large Pakistani companies |
| LinkedIn (organic) | Free support | Company page posts + team reposts build trust when people check the page after seeing an ad |

Meta is left out at this budget: it's cheap for views, but the people who sponsor or buy training programs are on LinkedIn.

## Audience (LinkedIn targeting)

- **Location:** Pakistan (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad / Rawalpindi)
- **Job functions / titles:** HR, Learning & Development, CSR, Sustainability, ESG, Corporate Communications, CEO / MD / Founder
- **Seniority:** Manager, Director, VP, CXO, Owner
- **Company size:** 200+ employees
- **Plus:** foundations, NGOs and development organizations
- **Retargeting:** people who watched 50%+ of any of our videos

## Frequency and when to change collaterals

### Schedule

| Week | Campaign 1 (awareness) | Campaign 2 (retargeting) | Organic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 16 Nov | Full video A + cutdowns B1, B2 | — | Post full video on company page |
| 2 · 23 Nov | Pause the weakest of the three; add B3 | — | Team reposts (2 to 3 people) |
| 3 · 30 Nov | Pause the weakest; add B4 | Starts with the best cutdown (C) | Post one graduate cutdown |
| 4 · 7 Dec | Keep the two best running | Continues | Results / thank-you post |

- **Ads run every day.** Target: each person sees an ad 2 to 3 times a week
- **Organic:** 1 company page post per week (4 total), reposted by 2 to 3 team members

### When to swap a video (check every Monday)

Swap out or pause a video if any of these happen:

- **Frequency above 4** (same people seeing it too often): add a new cutdown or widen the audience
- **View rate falls by a third** compared with its first week: people are tired of it, rotate in the next cutdown
- **One video clearly behind** the others after 7 days: pause it and move its money to the winner

Don't change things in the first 3 to 4 days. LinkedIn needs that time to learn.

## Message rule (needs Nasir's sign-off)

- Requested line: "pioneering and only program in Pakistan that guarantees employment"
- LinkedIn rejects or can pull ads with claims we can't back up ("only", "first", "guarantees"), and a competitor could challenge it
- **Option A (as requested):** use it only if the training team can confirm (1) placement was guaranteed in the program terms, and (2) no other program in Pakistan offers this
- **Option B (recommended, safe):** "A pioneering program in Pakistan built to end in employment. Every graduate placed into an internship or job."
