# Mari Testimonial Video Ads · Paid Promotion Plan v1

For Ali Abbasi, to get Nasir's go-ahead. Once approved: add the new card to the ad account and follow finance's billing instructions.

Related: [[2026-10-29-xloop-xcelerate-linkedin-video-script-v3-mari-ppl]], [[2026-10-19-xloop-mari-cohort-linkedin-video-script]]

## Campaign at a glance

- **What:** Mari graduate testimonial videos + the program highlight video, run as paid ads
- **Goal:** show companies that xCelerate's program ends in careers, and bring in conversations for the next sponsored cohort or corporate training
- **Main message:** collaboration (Mari Energies × xCelerate by xLoop) and the employability outcome. Fostering careers and lives, not just training
- **Platform:** LinkedIn (main). Optional add-on below
- **Dates:** Mon 16 Nov to Sun 27 Dec 2026 (6 weeks). Starts one week after approval for card setup, uploads and LinkedIn ad review
- **Recommended budget:** USD 1,200 (about PKR 336,000)

## Platforms

| Platform | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn (paid)** | Main channel, ~100% of budget | Reaches the decision makers: HR, L&D, CSR heads and CEOs of large Pakistani companies |
| LinkedIn (organic) | Free support | Company page posts + team reposts build trust when people check the page after seeing an ad |
| Meta: Facebook / Instagram (optional) | Only if Nasir wants public reach | Cheap views and good for the program's public image, but weak for reaching corporate buyers. Mari's earlier Meta run was for student applications, a different goal |

## Audience (LinkedIn targeting)

- **Location:** Pakistan (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad / Rawalpindi first)
- **Job functions / titles:** HR, Learning & Development, CSR, Sustainability, ESG, Corporate Communications, CEO / MD / Founder
- **Seniority:** Manager, Director, VP, CXO, Owner
- **Company size:** 200+ employees
- **Plus:** foundations, NGOs and development organizations (industry targeting)
- **Retargeting:** people who watched 50%+ of any of our videos

## Campaign structure

### Phase 1 · Awareness (weeks 1 to 6)

- **Objective:** Video views
- **Ads:** highlight video + 3 testimonial videos, all running together. LinkedIn shows the best performer more
- **Budget:** USD 800 (about USD 19/day)

### Phase 2 · Retargeting (weeks 3 to 6)

- **Objective:** Lead generation (LinkedIn Lead Gen Form, so no website setup is needed)
- **Who sees it:** people who watched 50%+ in Phase 1
- **Ad:** the strongest testimonial + "Plan your next training program with xLoop" form
- **Budget:** USD 400 (about USD 14/day)

## Frequency

- **Ads:** run every day for 6 weeks
- **How often one person sees an ad:** target 2 to 3 times per week. If it goes above 4, widen the audience or swap the video
- **Creative refresh:** rotate in a new testimonial every 2 weeks so the audience doesn't get bored
- **Organic posts (free):** 1 company page post per week (6 total), each reposted by 2 to 3 team members
- **Reporting:** short weekly numbers update to Ali every Monday; final report on Mon 28 Dec

## Ad copy

### Message rule (needs Nasir's sign-off)

- Requested line: "pioneering and only program in Pakistan that guarantees employment"
- LinkedIn rejects or can pull ads with claims we can't back up ("only", "first", "guarantees"), and a competitor could challenge it
- **Option A (as requested):** use it only if the training team can confirm (1) placement was guaranteed in the program terms, and (2) no other program in Pakistan offers this
- **Option B (recommended, safe):** "A pioneering program in Pakistan built to end in employment. Every graduate placed into an internship or job."

### Ad 1 · Highlight video

- **Intro text:** Training that doesn't stop at a certificate. 44 graduates from underserved communities, every one placed into an internship or a job. The Mari Digital Skills, Training & Employment Program is a collaboration between Mari Energies and xCelerate by xLoop, built to foster careers and lives, not just skills.
- **Headline:** 44 Graduates. Every One Placed Into Work.
- **CTA button:** Learn more

### Ads 2 to 4 · Testimonials

- **Intro text:** Five months ago, [first name] was [one line from the testimonial]. Today, [role] at [company, if consented]. This is what the Mari Digital Skills, Training & Employment Program, a collaboration between Mari Energies and xCelerate by xLoop, set out to do: careers, not just training.
- **Headline:** From Classroom to Career in Five Months
- **CTA button:** Learn more

### Ad 5 · Retargeting lead form

- **Intro text:** Planning a CSR program or training for your team? We design programs around the outcome you need. Mari's cohort: 44 graduates, all placed into internships and employment.
- **Headline:** Build Your Next Training Program With xLoop
- **Lead form fields:** name, company, job title, work email, "What are you planning?" (CSR cohort / team training / other)
