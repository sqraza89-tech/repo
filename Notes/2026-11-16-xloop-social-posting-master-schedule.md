## Standing rules

- Design needs **2 to 3 working days** (video longer). Brief Omais at least 3 working days before the post date
- Company page posts 10:00 PKT, ambassadors 09:30 PKT. Links always in the **first comment**
- CTAs go to a service or industry page, never the homepage (exception: MN08 is about the homepage itself)
- Every Tuesday, People & Culture asks staff to reshare the company post of the week (Advocacy tab)
- One company page post a day. When two plans collide, the CEO-requested campaign wins and the calendar post moves
