### 2. What we collect

**Information you give us**
- **Inquiry forms** (Contact page, blog sidebar, footer banner, service pages): name, work email, company, job title if you add it, and your message or topic
- **Assessment tools** (Data & AI Readiness Assessment, AI Security Readiness Assessment): your answers, and, if you request the full report, your first name, work email, company, company size and country
- **Calls you book with us:** the details you enter in the booking form
- **Emails you send us:** your email address and the content of your message

**Information collected automatically**
- **Usage data:** pages viewed, time on page, referring page, device type, browser and approximate location from your IP address, collected through Google Analytics (see section 6)
- **Security data:** information used by our spam protection to tell people from bots [CONFIRM: reCAPTCHA or Turnstile, as built]

We don't ask for sensitive personal information, and we ask you not to include it in forms or assessment answers.

### 3. How we use it

| Purpose | Information used | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Answer your inquiry and arrange calls you request | Form details, messages, booking details | Your request (steps before a contract) |
| Score your assessment and prepare your results and report | Assessment answers and report form details | Your request |
| Send the follow up email with your report, and one email about booking a review | Report form details | Your request, and our legitimate interest in following up |
| Understand how the website is used and improve it | Usage data | Your consent to analytics cookies |
| Protect the website from spam and abuse | Security data | Our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure |
| Meet legal obligations | Any of the above, as required | Legal obligation |

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

**Assessment answers** are stored and used only to score your assessment and prepare your diagnosis and report, and to prepare for a review call if you book one.

- **Authorities**, when the law requires it

xLoop works across several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including in [CONFIRM: countries where data is stored or accessed, e.g. the United States, the UAE and Pakistan]. We use:

You can change your choice at any time through [CONFIRM: "Cookie settings" link in the footer]. Declining analytics cookies doesn't affect how the site works.
