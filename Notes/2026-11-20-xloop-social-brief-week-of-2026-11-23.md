### IS11 Multi-cloud for AI: when it makes sense
- **Account + time:** Irfan Shaikh, 09:30 PKT · **Format:** Text only · **Design:** No
- **Caption:**
  > Multi-cloud for AI: when does it make sense?
  >
  > It helps when:
  > → A specific AI service only exists on another cloud
  > → Regulation or clients require data to stay in a particular region
  > → You already run large workloads in both
  >
  > It hurts when you do it "just in case": two sets of skills, two security models, and data moving between clouds at a cost.
  >
  > Start with one cloud done well. Add a second when there's a clear reason.

- **Hashtags:** #MultiCloud #CloudArchitecture
