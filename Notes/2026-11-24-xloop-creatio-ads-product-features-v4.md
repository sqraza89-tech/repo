## Ad formula

Every visual has four parts:

1. **Product name** (small, above the headline): e.g. "Creatio Lending"
2. **Punch line** (big, 8 words or fewer)
3. **4 feature ticks** (2 to 4 words each), named the way Creatio names them
4. **CTA strip** (unchanged): "Book a Creatio call for your bank." + Huzaifa Valika, VP Enterprise Sales · 0322 2009713

Footer on every visual: "Creatio × xLoop · Implemented in Pakistan by xLoop".
