## What the feedback means

- Half the ads lead with **market statistics** (92% digital payments, 132M accounts, $300M investment,
  0.7% of GDP, 1M claims). They describe Pakistan's market, not what Creatio does
- Huzaifa wants the **product** to be the headline: what a bank or insurer gets
- **Fix:** every visual leads with one product punch line. Market facts move into the post text as
  supporting proof, not the headline
- B2, B6, B7 and B8 are already product-led, so they stay with small tightening

## Rules for the punch lines

- Big text: 8 words or fewer, names a product capability
- Subline: one line on how it works, ending with "Implemented by xLoop" where it fits
- No numbers we can't source, no speed claims ("in minutes", "50% faster")
- Every capability below is on Creatio's own banking pages: onboarding, marketing, sales, service and
  contact center workflows; underwriting and verification; 360° customer view; omnichannel; teller and
  agent workplaces; no-code; AI agents working with people; cloud or on-premises deployment
