## 5. AI agents · `#ai-agents`

**H2:** How Do Creatio's AI Agents Work in a Bank?

Creatio's AI agents take on routine, repeatable steps inside your workflows, and your staff approve every decision that affects a customer, a loan or a complaint. xLoop sets them up on your data, inside the approval rules your risk and compliance teams agree.

*Design: agent cards in a row (scroll on mobile).*

| Agent | What it does | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer Onboarding** | Checks customer data and pre verifies documents within your KYC and AML workflow | Your team approves every account |
| **Loan Preparation** | Gathers and checks what a loan file needs before it reaches a credit officer | Your credit team makes the decision |
| **Referral** | Spots customers who fit another product and routes them to the right team | Your relationship managers follow up |
| **Renewal** | Flags policies, deposits and facilities coming up for renewal and prepares the next step | Your team confirms the offer |
| **Retention** | Identifies customers showing signs of leaving so someone can act in time | Your team decides how to respond |

**Line below:** Start without agents if you prefer. Most banks begin with the CRM and add agents once their data is in one place.

## 9. FAQ · `#faq`

**H2:** Creatio Implementation FAQ

*All six go into FAQPage schema, with text identical to the page.*

**Do we have to replace our core banking system to use Creatio?**
No. Creatio sits alongside your core banking system. xLoop connects the two so account data shows inside the CRM, and your core stays the system of record.

**Can Creatio run on premises?**
Yes. Creatio can run in the cloud or on premises. We agree the deployment model with your risk and security teams before we start, so customer data stays where your rules require.

**Can we start with the CRM and add AI agents later?**
Yes, and most banks do. Getting customer data into one place first makes the agents more useful when you add them.

**How long does a Creatio implementation take?**
It depends on how many teams, products and integrations are in scope. We give you a plan with dates after the discovery step. [CONFIRM typical range with delivery]

**Do AI agents make decisions on their own?**
No. The agents handle routine steps such as checking documents and preparing files. Your staff approve every decision on accounts, credit and complaints, and every agent action is logged.

**Is Creatio only for banks?**
No. Creatio is used across financial services. In Pakistan we work with banks, microfinance banks and insurers, and set it up for the products and processes each one runs.
