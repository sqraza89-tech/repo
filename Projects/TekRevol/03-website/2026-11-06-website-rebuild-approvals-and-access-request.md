# Website rebuild: approvals, verification and access

## Why this document

We are rebuilding the full TekRevol website, following the healthcare landing page. The new site targets **SMB and mid-market companies** rather than founders. Its job is to bring in more qualified leads, build a steady pipeline, and turn more of those leads into clients.

Content and design are produced first. The developer joins once every page has final copy and approved designs, so the build runs without waiting on input.

To keep that schedule, marketing needs three things from TekRevol:

1. **Approvals:** decisions on direction, structure and what we publish
2. **Verification:** facts, numbers and claims checked before they go live
3. **Access and assets:** data, tools and files to build and measure the site

Anything that isn't confirmed ships without the claim. Nothing unverified goes live.

## A. Approvals

Each item has our recommendation. A simple "approved" is enough, or tell us what to change.

| # | Decision | Recommendation | Reply |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Target audience | SMB and mid-market companies. Founders are no longer a target; founder pages (MVP, startup prototype) stay live for search traffic but leave the main menu and homepage | |
| A2 | Company size bands | SMB: 20 to 199 employees (about $5M to $50M revenue). Mid-market: 200 to 2,000 employees (about $50M to $1B revenue). Sales to adjust if deals close in a different band | |
| A3 | Five service areas | Custom software development · Web and mobile apps · Modernization and integration · AI and automation · Teams and support | |
| A4 | Main menu | Services · Industries · Work · How we work · Pricing · Company · button "Start a project" | |
| A5 | Launch pages (about 31) | Homepage, 5 service pages, 8 capability pages, pricing, security and compliance, 4 industry pages (healthcare, manufacturing, energy, retail), case studies, how we work, about, contact. Existing pages stay live; no URLs move at launch | |
| A6 | Calls to action | One main button: **Start a project**. Two supporting offers: **Systems review** (30 minutes on one workflow or system, with written next steps) and **Business case pack** (a one-page summary buyers can share internally) | |
| A7 | Publishing price ranges | Show planning ranges on the site, with a fixed quote after scoping. Buyers trust firms that show prices, and it filters out poor-fit leads | |
| A8 | Review process | One named approver for copy and design, with a 2 working day turnaround per review round | |
