## Class structure (Komal's Dynamik format)
1. **Warm-up** — full body, usually on the mat: cardio/pilates, simple yoga flow, or partner warm-up
2. **Aerial flow 1** — simple
3. **Drills** — core, grip or upper-body strength in the hammock (inversions, flips, straddle drills)
4. **Aerial flow 2**
5. **Partner pose**
6. **Savasana** — in the hammock or on the mat, student's choice

## How the two studios differ (decided 2026-10-12)
- **Dynamik:** 2 flows per class at intermediate–advanced level; drills = flips, inversions, landings, Instagram-style challenges
- **Asana:** 1 short flow (2 once they are ready); drills = confidence in inversions, first straddles, grip and core basics
- **Warm-up (both):** mat pilates and fast, fun full-body circuits. See `warmups.md`
- **Drills (both):** always with the hammock, for strength and practice. See `drills-and-tricks.md`
- **Savasana (both):** cocoon or mat cooldown moves, soothing music, stress-relief oil on wrists, a read-aloud message. See `savasana.md`
