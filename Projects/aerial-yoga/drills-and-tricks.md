# Drills, tricks and challenges (hammock-supported)

The drills block is for **strength and practice with the hammock**: 10–15 minutes.
Rule: I only teach what I can demo cleanly and spot. Tricks marked **(Komal first)** are ones I
must practise with Komal before she leaves, because I have not verified the steps myself.

Pictures: `visual-guide.html`.

## A. Strength drills (both studios)

| Drill | How | Asana | Dynamik |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active hang | Hold the fabric high, lift the feet, shoulders down | 5–10s ×3 | 20–30s ×3 |
| Seated pull-ups | Sit in the hammock, pull chest to hands | ×6 | ×10 |
| Tuck-ups | From a hang, knees to chest, lower slowly | ×3–5 | ×8 |
| Straddle-ups | From a hang, legs wide, hips lift to hands | intro only | ×5 |
| Aerial plank | Feet in the hammock, hands on the mat | 20s | 40s |
| Knee tucks | From aerial plank, knees to chest | ×8 | ×12 |
| Pike | From aerial plank, hips to the ceiling, legs straight | L3 only | ×8 |
| Aerial push-ups | Feet in the hammock, bend the elbows | knees down option | ×8 |
| Inverted straddle ↔ tuck | Upside down, open and close the legs | ×5 | ×10 |
| Inverted crunch | Upside down, hands behind the head, curl toward the knees | ×5 | ×10 |
| Hammock rows | Hold the fabric, lean back with a straight body, pull chest to hands | ×8 | ×12 |
| Single-leg squat | One foot in the hammock behind, squat on the standing leg | ×6 each | ×10 each |

## B. Inversions ladder
1. Flying / Superman (front hip crease)
2. Front hip hang (fold forward, legs wrap)
3. **Basic inversion / back straddle** (fabric at the sacrum, butterfly legs)
4. Inverted straddle, inverted split, hands free
5. Inverted pigeon / figure-4 (one ankle across the opposite thigh)
6. Straddle-up from a hang (invert without sitting first): Dynamik
7. Double knee hang **(Komal first)**: Dynamik

## C. Flips and landings (Dynamik; Asana only where marked)

| Trick | What it looks like | Needs first | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pull up to sitting | From the basic inversion, climb the fabric back to a seat | basic inversion | both studios |
| Front hip hang to feet | From the hip hang, hands to the mat, legs unwrap, step down | flying | both studios |
| Back roll-out (back flip dismount) | From the seat, hold the fabric, roll backward through a tuck to land on the feet | tuck-ups, basic inversion | **(Komal first)** |
| Skin the cat | From a hang, tuck and rotate backward through the arms, then return | tuck-ups ×5, active hang 20s | **(Komal first)** |
| Forward flip (360) | Fabric at the front hips, hold the fabric, somersault forward around it to the feet | flying, front hip hang, strong grip | **(Komal first)** |
| Hip key | A sideways wrap that locks the fabric around one hip | straddle-up | **(Komal first)** |
| Beginner drops | Controlled release from a wrap into a catch | everything above + crash mat | **(Komal first)**, Block 3 at the earliest |

Landing rules I say every time: "Eyes open. Hold the fabric until your feet are on the mat.
Land soft, knees bent."

## D. Challenges (the fun bit)
- **Hang-off:** everyone hangs; last one hanging wins. Track times on the skill card
- **Song challenge:** plank or hang during a song; on the repeated word do a tuck-up or a pull-up
- **Pull-up ladder:** 1 pull-up, rest, 2, rest, up to 5
- **Mirror flow:** partners copy each other's 3-pose flow
- **Flow in 60 seconds:** each student links any 4 poses; the class cheers
- **Trick of the week from Instagram:** I bring one saved reel, we break it into steps and try the entry-level version

## E. What Instagram is showing right now (checked 2026-10-12)
I could only see the public "popular" pages for #aerialhammock and #aerialyoga without logging
in, and I can read captions but not watch videos. What came up:

- **Drops dominate the big-view reels** (bungee drops, back dives with a twist). These are
  circus-level skills. The most-viewed creator herself warns they need professional coaching,
  safety rigging and years of practice. **Not for class.**
- **"Intermediate flow" reels set to music** from studios promoting classes: this is the format
  to copy for Dynamik's Flow 2
- **Song-based strength challenges** (a pull-up challenge to the Cha Cha Slide): easy to run in class
- **Hip key technique debates** among aerial teachers: a skill to learn with Komal
- **First-timer reactions and "why every woman should try aerial yoga" benefit lists**: good
  content ideas for @sanadefiesgravity, especially for Asana
