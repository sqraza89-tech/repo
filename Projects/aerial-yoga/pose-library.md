# Pose library — names, cues, levels

Names vary between schools (some brands trademark their own). Pick one name per pose, use it
every class, and students learn it fast. Format for each pose:
**Setup** · **Cues** (what to say) · **Watch for** · **Easier ↓ / Harder ↑**

Body-part words used below: *hip crease* (where the thigh meets the hip, front), *sit bones*,
*shoulder blades*, *sternum* (breastbone), *tailbone*, *ribs*, *core* (deep belly + back).

---

## 1. Mat warm-up (no hammock)
- **Cat–cow**: "Inhale, drop the belly, lift the gaze. Exhale, round the spine, chin to chest"
- **Downward dog** (Adho Mukha Svanasana): "Press the floor away, lift the sit bones, heels heavy"
- **Low lunge** (Anjaneyasana): "Back knee down, hips sink forward, lift the chest"
- **Plank → knee taps / shoulder taps**: "Shoulders over wrists, belly lifted, hips still"
- **Hollow body hold**: "Low back glued to the mat, arms and legs long, low and lifted" — key core shape for aerial
- **Wrist + shoulder circles, scapula push-ups**: grip and shoulder prep, never skip before hanging
- **Partner warm-up ideas**: back-to-back squats, partner row (hold hands, sit back), high-five plank

## 2. Standing poses with the hammock (hammock at hip height)

**Aerial hamstring stretch**
- Setup: heel in the hammock, standing leg straight, face the hammock
- Cues: "Square your hips. Lengthen your spine first, then hinge from the hips. Flex the foot"
- Watch for: rounding the back to reach further
- ↓ Bend the standing knee · ↑ Fold forward, hands to the floor

**Aerial lunge / Warrior 1**
- Setup: top of the back foot in the hammock, front foot on the floor in a lunge
- Cues: "Front knee over ankle. Tuck the tailbone, feel the front of the back hip open. Arms up, ribs soft"
- ↓ Hands on the front thigh · ↑ Back-bend, reach behind

**Standing pigeon**
- Setup: front shin across the hammock, standing leg on the floor
- Cues: "Flex the front foot to protect the knee. Square the hips, then fold slowly over the shin"
- Watch for: pain in the knee → come out (stretch should be felt in the hip/glute)

**Aerial chair**
- Setup: hammock behind you at mid-back, hold the fabric, sit back as if into a chair
- Cues: "Weight in the heels, knees over ankles, lift the chest"

**Supported backbend**
- Setup: hammock across the upper/mid back (below the shoulder blades), hold the fabric
- Cues: "Walk the feet forward, lean back, lead with the heart. Let the head follow last — don't drop it"
- ↓ Small lean, keep holding · ↑ Arms release overhead

**Aerial downward dog** (spinal decompression)
- Setup: step into the hammock, fabric across the front hip creases
- Cues: "Hinge forward, hands to the floor, walk them out. Let the hammock hold your hips — the spine grows longer"
- Students love this: "Let gravity do the work"

## 3. Flying poses (hammock across the front hip creases)

**Flying / Superman**
- Setup: fabric at the hip creases, hold the fabric, lean forward until the feet lift
- Cues: "Hips heavy, chest lifting. Squeeze the legs together and reach through the toes. When you're steady, release one hand… then the other. Arms like wings"
- Watch for: fabric on the belly (uncomfortable) → move it lower to the hip creases
- ↓ Toes touch the floor, keep holding · ↑ Arms forward, "flying" with a back-bend

## 4. Core + strength in the hammock

**Aerial plank**
- Setup: lie face down, shins/feet in the hammock behind you, walk the hands out to plank
- Cues: "Shoulders over wrists. Belly lifted — don't let the hips sag"
- Progressions: knee tucks → pike (hips to the ceiling) → push-ups

**Active hang** (grip foundation)
- Setup: hold the fabric above head height, feet on the floor, then lift them
- Cues: "Pull the shoulders away from the ears — like tucking them into your back pockets. Arms straight but alive"
- Build: 5s → 10s → 20s over weeks

**Tuck-ups / Straddle-ups**
- Setup: from an active hang
- Cues: "Draw the knees to the chest, slow and controlled. Lower like you're lowering into warm water"
- ↑ Straddle-up: "Open the legs wide and lift the hips to the hands" — the drill that leads to inverting from a hang

**Seated pull-ups**
- Setup: sit in the hammock, hold the fabric above, lift and lower

## 5. Seated poses in the hammock (hammock lower, sit fully inside)
- **Seated straddle / star**: "Open the legs to the edges, sit tall, hold the fabric beside you"
- **Mermaid**: one leg bent forward, one back, side-bend away from the back leg
- **Butterfly**: soles together, knees open, fold over

## 6. Inversions (only after screening — see safety.md)

**Basic inversion** (the foundation of everything upside down)
- Setup: fabric across the front hip creases, hands holding the fabric at chest/face height
- Teach the exit first: "To come up: hold the fabric, bend the knees, chin to chest, curl up"
- Cues, step by step:
  1. "Hold the fabric firmly, thumbs wrapped around"
  2. "Lean back slowly — the hammock stays in your hip creases"
  3. "Lift one foot, then the other, and bring the soles together around the fabric, knees wide"
  4. "Let your head go heavy. Breathe. You're hanging from your hips — your legs are just keeping you secure"
- Watch for: fabric slipping up to the waist/ribs (reset lower), holding the breath, panic → calm voice, "I'm right here, hold the fabric, bend the knees, curl up"

**Variations from the basic inversion**
- **Inverted butterfly**: soles together, knees open (the default resting shape)
- **Inverted straddle (V)**: legs open wide outside the fabric, feet flexed
- **Inverted split**: one leg forward, one back
- **Inverted lotus**: only for students with easy lotus on the floor
- **Hands free / reach for the floor**: once the legs are secure, arms hang, palms to the mat

**Exits**
- **Curl up**: the safest — hold the fabric, bend the knees, chin to chest, curl up
- **Roll-down / come to the floor**: hands to the mat, release the legs one at a time, walk out (hammock height must allow it)

## 7. Advanced (Dynamik, once the base is solid)
- **Skin the cat / back flip through the arms**: from an active hang, tuck, rotate backward through the arms to inverted, then (only if the room allows) down to the feet. Needs strong tuck-ups first. Spot closely
- **Inverted straddle to tuck and back** (core drill upside down)
- **Flows linking 3–4 shapes**: e.g. flying → inversion → straddle → curl up

## 8. Partner poses
- **Partner flying**: one flies, the partner holds their hands in front and gently pulls for a longer stretch
- **Counterbalance backbend**: both hold each other's wrists in supported backbends, leaning away
- **Mirror flow**: partners face each other and match a 3-pose flow
- **Seated partner straddle (mat)**: hold hands, one leans back as the other folds forward

## 9. Savasana
- **Cocoon**: sit in the hammock, open the fabric wide, lie back fully wrapped, gentle swing
- **Mat savasana**: legs up in the hammock (calves resting) is a lovely middle option
