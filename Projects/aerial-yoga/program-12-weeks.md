# 12-week program — Nov 2026 to Jan 2027

Three 4-week blocks. Each block has one **skill of the month** (with 3 levels), one **strength**
focus and one **mobility** focus. Dynamik pushes the higher levels; Asana stays mostly at
Levels 1–2 with longer stretches and more savasana.

Every class keeps the same order (warm-up → flow 1 → drills → flow 2 → partner → savasana), so
students feel safe, but the content changes.

## Block 1 — Foundations & trust (weeks 1–4, November)
- **Goal:** students trust the hammock and they trust me (handover period)
- **Skill of the month: the basic inversion**
  - L1: flying with toes on the floor, hands holding
  - L2: basic inversion, inverted butterfly, curl-up exit
  - L3: inverted straddle + split, hands free
- **Strength:** active hang (5–10s), aerial plank, hollow body
- **Mobility:** hips — lunge, standing pigeon, butterfly
- **Week 4:** "show your level" — everyone does their level of the skill in the flow

## Block 2 — Strength & control (weeks 5–8, December)
- **Goal:** students feel stronger and can feel it in everyday life
- **Skill of the month: tuck-up → straddle-up**
  - L1: seated pull-ups, active hang 10s
  - L2: tuck-ups from the hang ×5, controlled down
  - L3: straddle-up toward inversion from a hang
- **Strength:** grip (hangs, holds), aerial plank → knee tucks → pike
- **Mobility:** hamstrings + shoulders — hamstring stretch, downward dog in the hammock, shoulder openers
- **Week 8:** strength check — compare the hang time with week 1

## Block 3 — Flow & flight (weeks 9–12, January)
- **Goal:** linking shapes into a flow that feels like dancing in the air
- **Skill of the month: a 4-pose aerial flow** (flying → inversion → straddle → curl up)
  - L1: the flow without the inversion (flying → backbend → seated)
  - L2: the full flow with pauses
  - L3: the flow without pauses + skin the cat (Dynamik, spotted, only if tuck-ups are solid)
- **Strength:** core upside down (straddle ↔ tuck while inverted)
- **Mobility:** spine — supported backbend, mermaid, twists
- **Week 12:** showcase class. Everyone performs the flow at their level; film it with consent and compare with week 1

## Making the progress visible
- **Skill card** (paper or a story highlight): each student ticks off L1, L2, L3 for every skill
- **Recap at the end of each block:** "Four weeks ago ___. Today ___."
- **Celebrate the first time** (first inversion, first straddle-up): name it in class, and post it if they agree

## Weekly rhythm (if 2–3 Dynamik classes)
- Class A: skill introduction + drills
- Class B: same skill, deeper + a new flow
- Class C (if there is one): mobility-heavy + skill repetition + a fun partner challenge
