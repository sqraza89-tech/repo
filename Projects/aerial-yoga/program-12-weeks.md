# 12-week program — Nov 2026 to Jan 2027 (two tracks)

The two studios run **separate tracks**. Same class order, different level.

| | Asana Wellness (beginner) | Dynamik (intermediate–advanced) |
|---|---|---|
| Where they are now | Basic inversions done; 1–2 can straddle | Many flows done; ready for tricks |
| Warm-up | Mat pilates + blocks/weights, gentler pace | Mat pilates + light weights, fast circuits |
| Flows per class | 1 short flow (add a 2nd from Block 2) | **2 flows**, intermediate–advanced |
| Drills | Confidence, grip, first straddles | Flips, inversions, landings, challenges |
| Savasana | Longer (8–10 min), full ritual | 5 min, ritual lite |

Pose names: see `pose-library.md` and `drills-and-tricks.md`. Pictures: `visual-guide.html`.

## Track A — Asana Wellness

### Block 1 (weeks 1–4, Nov): Confident upside down
- **Skill of the month: basic inversion, owned**
  - L1: flying with toes down; front hip hang with hands on the mat
  - L2: basic inversion with butterfly legs, pull up to sitting unaided
  - L3: inverted straddle, hands free
- **Strength:** active hang 5–10s, aerial plank, seated pull-ups
- **Mobility:** hips (aerial lunge, standing pigeon, butterfly)
- **Flow (1 per class, 3–4 poses):** hamstring stretch → aerial lunge → flying → seat

### Block 2 (weeks 5–8, Dec): Strong hands, strong core
- **Skill of the month: straddle for everyone**
  - L1: seated straddle in the hammock, active hang 10s
  - L2: inverted straddle from the basic inversion, hold 5 breaths
  - L3: inverted straddle ↔ tuck ×5, inverted split
- **Strength:** tuck-ups from a hang, knee tucks in aerial plank
- **Mobility:** hamstrings + shoulders
- **Flows:** flow 1 as before; add a short flow 2 with the inversion in it

### Block 3 (weeks 9–12, Jan): First real flow
- **Skill of the month: a 4-pose flow** (flying → seat → basic inversion → inverted straddle → pull up)
  - L1: without the inversion · L2: with pauses · L3: smooth, to music
- **Strength:** aerial plank → pike; hang 15–20s
- **Mobility:** spine (supported backbend, mermaid)
- **Week 12:** showcase class, filmed with consent

## Track B — Dynamik

Every class: **Flow 1 (learn) → drills → Flow 2 (perform)**. Flow 2 uses the trick drilled that day.

### Block 1 (weeks 1–4, Nov): Clean lines
- **Trick of the month: straddle-up (invert from a hang)**
  - L1: tuck-ups ×5 with control · L2: straddle-up with a small jump · L3: straight-arm straddle-up, slow down
- **Drills:** active hang 20s, tuck-ups, inverted straddle ↔ tuck, pike in aerial plank
- **Landings:** back roll-out to standing from the seat; front hip hang to feet
- **Flows:** 5–6 poses each. Flow 2 adds the inversion + one variation
- **Challenge of the week:** e.g. longest hang, pull-up song challenge (see `drills-and-tricks.md`)

### Block 2 (weeks 5–8, Dec): Flips
- **Trick of the month: skin the cat → forward flip (360)**
  - L1: skin the cat halfway and back · L2: full skin the cat to feet · L3: forward flip in the hammock, controlled landing
- **Drills:** hollow body, tuck-ups ×8, single-leg balance landings
- **Flows:** 6–8 poses; flow 2 includes a flip
- **Challenge of the week:** recreate a saved Instagram flow (Sana brings one per week)

### Block 3 (weeks 9–12, Jan): Signature flow
- **Trick of the month: one new trick Komal has already taught me** (hip key, knee hang or a beginner drop: only what I can demo and spot)
- **Flows:** one long choreographed flow to music, built over 4 weeks (8–10 poses)
- **Week 12:** showcase. Film it; students post their own

## Making progress visible (both studios)
- **Skill card** per student: tick L1/L2/L3 for each month's skill
- **Recap at the end of each block:** "Four weeks ago ___. Today ___."
- **Strength check:** hang time in week 1, 4, 8 and 12
- **First times are celebrated** in class, and posted only if they agree
