# Savasana — cooldown moves, rituals and read-aloud messages

## 1. Order of the last 8–10 minutes
1. **Cooldown moves** (3–4 min): pick 3 from the list below, soft music on
2. **Settle** (30s): "Find your final position. Cocoon or mat, your choice"
3. **Read the message** (about 5 min, slowly), walking around: oil on wrists, a gentle swing
4. **Silence** (1 min)
5. **Wake up** and close

**Ask before touching, every class:** "During rest I come around with a little oil for your
wrists and a gentle swing. If you'd rather not be touched today, cross your ankles and I'll
pass by." Check for allergies and pregnancy before using any essential oil.

## 2. Cooldown moves

### In the cocoon
| Move | How | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Cocoon butterfly | Lying inside, soles together, knees open | "Let the knees fall, the fabric holds them" |
| Knees to chest | Hug both knees, rock side to side | "Give yourself a hug" |
| Cocoon twist | Knees to one side, arms wide, then switch | "Wring out the day" |
| Figure-4 | Ankle over the opposite knee, draw in | "Soft hips, soft jaw" |
| Full-length stretch | Arms overhead, toes long, then release | "Longest you've been all day" |
| Side-lying curl | Curl up on one side like sleeping | "Small and safe" |
| Gentle swing | Push off once with a hand, then be still | "Let it slow by itself" |

### On the mat
| Move | How | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Legs up in the hammock | On the back, calves resting in the fabric | "Let the legs be heavy. The hammock carries them" |
| Child's pose | Knees wide, forehead down, arms long | "Forehead heavy on the mat" |
| Supine twist | Knees to one side, look the other way | "Both shoulders melting down" |
| Happy baby | Hold the feet, knees wide, rock | "Rock like you did as a baby" |
| Seated forward fold | Legs long, fold, hold the fabric for a gentle pull | "Fold from the hips, not the shoulders" |
| Supported fish (Asana) | Block under the upper back, arms open | "Open the heart to the ceiling" |
| Wrist and forearm release | Shake out, circle, stretch the fingers back | "Thank your hands. They held you today" |

## 3. Rituals: sustainable, cheap, easy

| Idea | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Stress-relief oil on wrists** | Low | One roller bottle lasts weeks. Lavender diluted in a carrier oil. Ask first |
| **Cold towels** (Komal's) | Low | Small face towels, damp, in a cooler box with a few drops of rose water or mint. Wash and reuse |
| **Warm towels in winter** | Low | Same towels, hot water in a flask, wring out. Lovely Dec–Jan |
| **Rose water face mist** | Very low | One spray bottle. Two sprays above the cocoon as you pass |
| **Affirmation cards** | One-time | Print 30 cards. Each student draws one on the way out |
| **A single date or a small sweet** | Low | Left on the mat for when they wake. Fits the tea ritual at Asana |
| **Eye pillows** | One-time | Small fabric bags filled with rice and dried lavender. Easy to sew, last for years |
| **Singing bowl or chime** | One-time | One soft ring to end the rest. Becomes your signature sound |
| **Gentle hammock swing** | Free | One slow push per student |
| **Head or shoulder press** | Free | 10 seconds, with consent. Wash hands or use sanitizer between students |
| **"One word" circle** | Free | Before leaving, each student says one word for how they feel |
| **Skill-card stamp** | Very low | A small sticker when someone levels up |

Suggested signature: **oil on wrists + one chime + an affirmation card on the way out.**
Cheap, quick, and nobody else does all three.

## 4. Read-aloud messages (about 5 minutes each)

Read slowly. Pause for a full breath at every line break. "…" means a longer pause.

### Message 1 — You showed up
You made it here today.

Whatever your day looked like before this hour, you chose to come. That choice matters more than any pose.

Let your body be heavy now. Let the hammock, or the mat, hold all of your weight.
You don't have to hold anything up anymore.
…
Feel your feet soften. Your ankles. Your calves.
Feel your knees release, and your thighs grow heavy.
Let your hips sink. Let your belly be soft. Nobody is looking. Let it rise and fall.
…
Think of your hands. Today they gripped, they pulled, they held you in the air.
Let them open now. Let the fingers curl however they want.
…
Soften your shoulders away from your ears.
Unclench your jaw. Let your tongue rest.
Smooth the space between your eyebrows.
…
So much of your day is spent taking care of other people. Answering, carrying, planning, remembering.
This moment is not for anyone else.
This breath is yours.
…
You don't have to be productive right now.
You don't have to be strong right now.
You already were.
…
Notice your breath, without changing it.
In… and out.
In… and out.
…
If a thought comes, let it pass like a cloud. You don't need to follow it.
…
Remember this: your body did things today that once felt impossible.
It can do more than you think. And so can you.
…
Stay here. Rest. I'll bring you back in a moment.

### Message 2 — Letting go
Take a deep breath in through your nose…
and let it out slowly through your mouth.

One more time. Breathe in…
and let it all go.
…
Imagine that with every breath out, something you've been carrying slides off your shoulders.
A worry about tomorrow. Let it go.
Something someone said. Let it go.
The list in your head. It will still be there later. For now, let it go.
…
Feel the support underneath you.
You are completely held.
You cannot fall.
…
Today you trusted a piece of fabric to hold you upside down.
That is not a small thing.
Trust is a muscle too. You trained it today.
…
Let your legs be heavy.
Let your arms be heavy.
Let your head be heavy.
…
There is nothing to fix right now.
Nothing to solve.
Nowhere to be.
…
Feel your heartbeat. Steady. Loyal. It has been working for you every second of your life, asking nothing back.
Send it a little thank you.
…
With every breath in, imagine a soft light filling your chest.
With every breath out, it spreads. Down your arms. Down your legs. Up to the top of your head.
…
You are allowed to rest.
You are allowed to take up space.
You are allowed to do something just because it makes you feel alive.
…
Stay with that feeling. I'll bring you back gently.

### Message 3 — Strong and soft
You are stronger today than you were last week.

Maybe you held on a second longer. Maybe you tried something that scared you. Maybe you just came, on a day when it was hard to come.
All of it counts.
…
Now let that strength rest.
A muscle grows when it rests, not when it works.
So this part of class is not extra. This is where the change happens.
…
Relax your toes.
Relax your legs.
Relax your hips and your lower back. Let them spread wide.
…
Relax your belly. Let it be round and soft.
We spend so much time holding it in. Not here.
…
Relax your chest, and feel it open.
Relax your shoulders, your arms, your wrists, your fingers.
…
Relax your neck.
Relax your face. Your cheeks. Your eyes behind your eyelids.
…
Being strong does not mean being hard.
The fabric that held you today is soft. And it carried your whole weight.
You can be like that. Soft, and able to carry a great deal.
…
Think of one thing your body did today that you're proud of.
Just one.
Hold it in your mind.
…
Say to yourself, silently: I did that.
…
And next week, you'll do a little more.
There is no hurry.
…
Breathe in… and out.
Rest here. I'll call you back soon.

### Message 4 — Coming home to yourself
Close your eyes, and come back home.

Home to your body. Home to your breath.
…
You've been everywhere today. In your phone. In your plans. In other people's needs.
Now come back to here.
…
Feel the places where your body touches the fabric or the mat.
The back of your head.
Your shoulder blades.
Your hips.
Your heels.
…
Feel the air on your skin.
Hear the music, and the quiet underneath it.
…
Nothing is missing in this moment.
You are warm. You are safe. You are breathing.
…
If you're in your cocoon, feel how it wraps around you.
It was made to hold one person. Right now, it holds you.
…
Let your breath slow down.
In, for a count of four. Two… three… four.
Out, for a count of six. Two… three… four… five… six.
Again. In… two… three… four.
Out… two… three… four… five… six.
…
Now let the counting go, and just breathe.
…
You don't have to earn rest.
You don't have to finish everything first.
Rest is part of the work.
…
When you leave here today, take this quiet with you.
In the car. In the kitchen. In the middle of a busy afternoon.
One slow breath, and you're home again.
…
Stay a little longer. I'll bring you back soon.

### Message 5 — Thank you, body
Let's end by saying thank you.

Thank you, feet, for carrying me here.
…
Thank you, legs, for every step, every stair, every time you held me steady.
…
Thank you, hips and back, for bending, for lifting, for turning upside down today.
…
Thank you, belly and core, for holding my centre, even when I shook.
…
Thank you, heart, for beating faster when I was brave, and slowing down now that I'm at rest.
…
Thank you, arms. Thank you, hands. You held on today.
You hold so much every day.
…
Thank you, shoulders, for everything you carry. You can put it down now.
…
Thank you, mind, for being willing to try something new. For saying "maybe I can".
…
We are often hard on our bodies. We notice what we'd like to change.
Today, notice what yours can do.
It flew today.
…
Take a slow breath in, and fill yourself with that thank you.
Breathe out, and let it settle into every part of you.
…
You are doing better than you think.
You are stronger than you know.
And you are allowed to be proud of yourself.
…
Rest in that. I'll bring you back in a moment.

## 5. Waking them up (after any message)
"Slowly start to deepen your breath.
Move your fingers. Move your toes.
Stretch your arms over your head, as long as you can.
If you're in your cocoon, open the fabric and sit up slowly. If you're on the mat, roll to your right side first.
Take your time. There's no rush.
Bring your hands to your heart.
Thank you for flying with me today. You were wonderful."
[one chime]
