# Teaching handbook — how to say it

Doing a trick and teaching it are two separate skills. Teaching is a set of habits you can
learn: a cue formula, a word bank, and a few scripts you know by heart.

## 1. The cue formula
**Where → Action → Feel/Breath**
- "**Hips** (where) **sink toward the floor** (action) — **feel the front of the thigh open** (feel)"
- "**Shoulders** — **draw them down your back** — **breathe into the space**"

Rules:
- **One cue at a time.** Say it, wait one breath, then the next
- **Breath leads movement:** "Inhale, lengthen… exhale, fold"
- **Positive over negative:** "Lift the belly" beats "don't sag"
- **Cue the body, not the student:** "Knees stay soft", not "Sara, your knees"
- **Repeat key cues 2–3 times.** Students only hear about half of what you say the first time

## 2. Teach a new skill in 5 steps
1. **Name it + why:** "This is flying. It opens the chest and strengthens the back"
2. **Demo once, silent or slow**, so they see the whole shape
3. **Exit first:** "Here's how you come out"
4. **Talk them in, step by step** (you stay on the floor, watching)
5. **Offer levels:** "Level 1 stay here. Level 2 release one hand. Level 3 both hands"

## 3. Word bank

**Action verbs:** lengthen · reach · press · root · lift · draw in · hug · soften · sink · melt ·
spiral · squeeze · float · wrap · open · hinge · curl · stack · extend · engage · release · glide

**Body parts to use:** hip creases · sit bones · tailbone · ribs · belly button · sternum / heart ·
collarbones · shoulder blades · crown of the head · inner thighs · soles of the feet · fingertips

**Imagery (makes cues stick):**
- "Shoulders tucked into your back pockets"
- "Zip up from the inner thighs to the belly"
- "Let the hammock hold you — you're being carried"
- "Hips heavy like sandbags, heart light like a balloon"
- "Lower like you're lowering into warm water"
- "Grow taller on every inhale"
- "Lead with your heart, let the head follow"
- "Feet like you're pressing into a wall"

**Pacing words:** "take your time" · "one more breath here" · "when you're ready" ·
"slowly on the way out" · "pause at the top"

## 4. Encouragement that builds confidence
- "Your body is stronger than you think"
- "Shaky is good — that's strength being built"
- "Fear is normal. Every one of us felt it the first time"
- "Look where you are right now — upside down. Two weeks ago that was a 'maybe'"
- "Notice how this felt different from last week"
- "Make it yours — take the level that feels right today"
- "Strong isn't how it looks. It's that you came back and tried again"
- Name the win specifically: "Amna, that straddle was controlled all the way down — beautiful"

## 5. Handling the room
- **Nervous student:** stand next to them, lower your voice. "Hold the fabric, I'm right here. We go only as far as you want." Let them stop at any step — that step is today's win
- **Mixed levels:** always give 3 levels. Advanced students hold longer or add the next shape while you help beginners
- **Someone goes off and does their own trick:** "Love the energy — let's all stay with the class flow for now, I'll give you a challenge at the end"
- **You forget the next pose:** "Take a breath here, feel where you are." (Buys 5 seconds.) Keep your plan card on the mat
- **Silence is fine.** You don't need to talk the whole time — let them breathe in a pose

## 6. Scripts

### Opening — Dynamik
"Welcome, everyone! For those who don't know me, I'm Sana — I've been training in this room for two
years, and I'm so excited to be teaching you. Quick check before we start: anyone new, or with an
injury, pregnancy, or anything I should know? Come tell me privately.
Today's focus is ___. By the end of class you'll ___. Let's get warm."

### Opening — Asana Wellness
"Welcome. Take your tea, find a comfortable seat. Close your eyes if you like… Take a slow breath
in… and a long breath out. Leave the day at the door. This hour is just for you.
Today we're working on ___ — gentle on the body, strong in the mind.
Before we start, if you have any injuries or health conditions, or if you're pregnant, let me know."

### Hook lines for the start of class
- "Today, we're going to fly."
- "By the end of this class, every one of you will be upside down — or one step closer."
- "Today is about strong hands, because strong hands give you freedom in the air."
- "We sit all day. Today, we hang and lengthen."
- "Last week was about getting up. This week is about staying there with control."

### Talking them into the basic inversion
"Find your hammock in your hip creases. Hold the fabric, thumbs wrapped.
First, the way out: bend your knees, chin to your chest, curl up. Remember that.
Now, slowly lean back. The hammock stays in your hips — trust it.
Lift one foot… then the other. Bring the soles together around the fabric, knees wide. Butterfly legs.
Let your head go. Breathe. You're upside down! Stay for three breaths.
Now — knees bend, chin to chest, curl up slowly. Sit in the hammock and take a breath before you stand."

### During drills (Dynamik)
"Three more. Make them slow — slow is strong… Two… Last one, best one… And rest. Shake out your hands."

### Savasana
"Climb into your hammock and open the fabric wide around you. Or lie on your mat with your legs
up in the hammock — your choice. Let yourself be held. Nothing to do now.
Feel your heartbeat slowing… your shoulders softening… your hands, which worked so hard, resting.
[2–5 min silence or soft music]
Begin to move your fingers and toes. Take your time coming out, and come to a seat."

### Closing
"Today you ___ (name the win). Remember how that felt. Thank you for flying with me — see you on ___."
At Asana: hand out cold towels while they're seated.

## 7. What makes classes different and keep people coming back
- **A peak skill every class**, and everything else builds toward it
- **Skill of the month** with 3 levels so everyone has a goal (see `program-12-weeks.md`)
- **Recap the win at the end** — accomplishment is felt when it's said out loud
- **Same structure, new content** — routine gives safety, novelty gives excitement
- **Learn names and remember last week**: "Hina, last week you got the inversion — today let's try the straddle"
- **Signature moments**: Dynamik = "challenge of the week" at the end; Asana = tea + intention at the start, cold towel at the end
- **Music**: upbeat for warm-up and drills, slower for flows, ambient for savasana
- **Progress record**: with consent, film a skill in week 1 and week 12
