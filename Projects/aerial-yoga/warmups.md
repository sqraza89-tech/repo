# Warm-ups — mat pilates + fast full-body circuits

10–12 minutes. Aim: heart rate up, core switched on, wrists and shoulders ready to hang.
Kit: **Dynamik** light weights · **Asana** blocks + weights. Pictures: `visual-guide.html`.

Always finish the warm-up with **wrist circles, shoulder circles and 5 scapula push-ups**.

## Exercise bank (names to learn)

### Mat pilates (core)
| Exercise | What it is | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| The Hundred | On the back, legs up at 45°, head lifted, arms pump | "Pump the arms. Inhale 5, exhale 5. Belly scooped" |
| Roll-up | Lie flat, peel up to reach the toes, roll back down | "One bone at a time, like peeling off the mat" |
| Single-leg stretch | On the back, head up, hug one knee, switch | "Switch, switch. Hips stay still" |
| Double-leg stretch | Hug both knees, then reach arms and legs long | "Reach long, circle, hug" |
| Scissors | Legs straight, one up one down, switch | "Pulse pulse, switch" |
| Criss-cross | Bicycle with a twist, elbow toward opposite knee | "Rotate from the ribs, not the elbows" |
| Glute bridge | Hips up, squeeze, lower slowly | "Press through the heels, ribs down" |
| Swimming | Face down, opposite arm and leg flutter | "Long and low, reach away" |
| Side-lying leg lifts | On the side, top leg lifts and lowers | "Hip stacked on hip" |
| Hollow hold | Low back down, arms and legs hover | "Low back glued to the mat" |
| Plank / side plank | Straight line, shoulders over wrists | "Push the floor away" |
| Bird-dog | All fours, opposite arm and leg reach | "Balance a cup of tea on your back" |

### Fast and fun (cardio, 30–40s each)
Jumping jacks · high knees · squat pulses · mountain climbers · skaters · plank jacks ·
inchworm walk-outs · fast feet · squat to calf raise · dance break (8 counts, their choice)

### With light weights (Dynamik + Asana)
Squat to overhead press · bent-over row · bicep curl to press · tricep kickback ·
weighted glute bridge · Russian twist with one weight · standing punches · lateral raise

### With blocks (Asana)
- Block between the thighs in bridge or chair pose: "squeeze the block" (inner thighs + pelvic floor)
- Block between the hands overhead in a lunge: shoulders switch on
- Block under the hands in a forward fold or lunge: brings the floor closer for beginners
- Block under the sacrum in a supported bridge: gentle backbend
- Block pass: lying down, pass the block from hands to feet (V-up pass)

### Partner warm-ups
Back-to-back squat · partner row (hold wrists, sit back) · high-five plank · mirror game
(one leads, one copies) · wheelbarrow hold (Dynamik only)

## Ready-made warm-ups

### W1 — "Pilates burn" (Asana, 10 min)
1. Cat–cow ×6 → downward dog, pedal the feet (1 min)
2. Glute bridge with a block between the thighs ×12 + hold 10s
3. The Hundred (legs bent for L1)
4. Single-leg stretch ×10 → criss-cross ×10
5. Bird-dog ×6 each side
6. Plank 20s → child's pose
7. Wrists, shoulders, 5 scapula push-ups

### W2 — "Fast and fun circuit" (Dynamik, 10 min)
40s on / 15s rest, 2 rounds:
1. Jumping jacks
2. Squat to overhead press (weights)
3. Mountain climbers
4. Bent-over row (weights)
5. Plank jacks
6. Hollow hold
Then wrists, shoulders, 5 scapula push-ups, 10s active hang.

### W3 — "Sculpt" (both, 12 min)
1. Inchworm walk-outs ×5
2. Squat pulses 30s → squat to calf raise ×10
3. Bicep curl to press ×12 → tricep kickback ×12
4. Russian twist ×20 → scissors ×20
5. Side plank 15s each side → swimming 20s
6. Wrists, shoulders, scapula push-ups

### W4 — "Partner party" (both, 10 min)
1. Mirror game 1 min
2. Back-to-back squat ×10
3. High-five plank ×10
4. Partner row ×10
5. Each alone: The Hundred + hollow hold 20s
6. Wrists, shoulders, scapula push-ups

### W5 — "Song challenge" (Dynamik, one song)
Pick a song with a repeated word. Hold a plank or squat; every time the word comes, do a
push-up or a jump. (Seen on Instagram as song-based strength challenges.)

Rotate W1–W5 so no two classes in a week start the same way.
