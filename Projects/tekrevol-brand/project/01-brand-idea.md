# 1. Brand idea and positioning

## What TekRevol is

**One line:** TekRevol builds the AI-native software other vendors, and other AI tools, couldn't finish.

**Category:** AI-first software development company. Software development stays the parent category (that is where the search demand is). AI moves from one service among many to the headline.

## The brand idea: Better with every layer

Many software companies now call themselves AI companies. What sets TekRevol apart is how it got here:

- **It adapted early.** TekRevol took up AI tools and methods ahead of most of its market, and folded them into how it builds.
- **It scaled fast.** It turned that early start into AI services, private-LLM work (RevAI) and AI-native delivery.
- **It keeps evolving.** Every project, every learning and every architecture becomes the base for the next one. The work gets better layer by layer.

The identity makes that growth visible. The logo is already built from stacked planes. The system extends it: **layers that stack and get brighter as they rise**, from deep Ember at the base to bright Flare at the top.

| Layer | What it stands for | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| Base (Ember) | Ten years of engineering: mobile, web, cloud, custom software | The foundation of every layout and gradient |
| Middle (Signal) | Applied AI and automation in real products | CTAs, highlights, the logo |
| Top (Flare, Glow) | What comes next: each new model, method and learning | Light in imagery, hover states, the peak of the Strata motif |

## Personality

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Built** | Engineered, precise, specific | Cold or robotic |
| **Quick** | Moves early, ships, communicates on schedule | Rushed or careless |
| **Evolving** | Always improving the next version | Restless or trend-chasing |
| **Candid** | Says what is true, including what went wrong before | Blunt or negative about others |

## Why orange matters now

Four of the five named competitors lead with blue: LeewayHertz, Azumo, ScienceSoft and Cheesecake Labs (also the reference site). HatchWorks uses teal. The AI services category reads as one blue wall. A brighter, more digital orange on warm carbon gives TekRevol instant recognition in that field, and it keeps the equity the brand already has.

## From the old brand to the new

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "Human Vision. Intelligent Technology. Exceptional Products." | A headline that names what TekRevol is and who it is for |
| Muted orange #F37A20 on generic dark photos | Signal Orange #FF5A1F with the Ascent gradient and Strata layers |
| Poppins everywhere | Unbounded (display), Archivo (text), JetBrains Mono (labels, code, data) |
| Rounded pill buttons | Square buttons with the notch |
| Stock "AI" imagery: glowing brains, blue networks | Real builders, real interfaces, layered light |

## Proof the idea needs

The brand idea is about speed of adoption, so it needs evidence before it goes into public claims. Collect:

- the year TekRevol started building with AI, and its first AI product or engagement
- how many AI or automation projects have shipped since
- RevAI milestones
- the Lovable partner listing, once it is live (not before)

Until these are confirmed, show the idea through design and through real work, and avoid number claims about speed.
