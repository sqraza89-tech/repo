# Logos

- `tekrevol-logo-refresh.svg`: full logo with brightened oranges (#FF5A1F, #FF8A3D, #E63A0E) and carbon #0F0D0C ink. For paper and white backgrounds. Proposed
- `tekrevol-logo-refresh-white.svg`: same, white ink. For carbon and dark imagery. Proposed
- `tekrevol-mark-refresh.svg` / `-white.svg`: the mark alone, for favicons, avatars and watermarks under 120px. Proposed
- `tekrevol-logo-current.svg` / `-white.svg`: the live legacy logo (#EF5123, #F37A20, #EE3824, ink #282526), kept for comparison only

Geometry is copied unchanged from the live tekrevol.com logo; only the fills differ. The SVGs are multi-color with fixed fills, so they do not follow CSS `color`.
