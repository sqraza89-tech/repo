# Card

Cards hold one service, case study or idea each: label, title, one or two lines, one link.

- `tr-card`: `surface-raised`, `radius-0`, padding `space-6` (`space-4` on mobile), `space-4` gap inside
- Title in the display face at `h3` size; body in `ink-muted`
- `tr-card--featured`: the one card that matters most in a row gets the `notch-md` cut and `glow-accent`. Only one per row
- Hover: `glow-accent` on carbon, `lift` on pearl
- Case study cards lead with the client's result, then the challenge

Do not: add colored left borders, round the corners, put two links or buttons in one card, or fill cards with orange.
