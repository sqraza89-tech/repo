## How to add a claim

1. State the claim in one sentence.
2. Name the source — document, page, project, or external publication with its authority level.
3. Assign an evidence class (see `08_governance/evidence_rules.md`).
4. Confirm public-use permission where a client is involved.
5. Get sign-off: Head of Marketing, plus the practice lead for any delivery or technical claim.
6. Add it here with a date.

**A claim published without going through this list is a governance failure, not a style choice.**
