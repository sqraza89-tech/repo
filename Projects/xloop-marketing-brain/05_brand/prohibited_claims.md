# Prohibited Claims

Never publish these. Some are prohibited because they are false, some because they are
unsubstantiated, and some because they belong to somebody else.

---

## 1. Certification and credential claims

| ❌ Never say | ✅ Say instead |
|---|---|
| "xLoop is ISO 27001 certified" | "Certified ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 Lead Auditors" (individual level) |
| "xLoop is ISO 42001 certified" | As above |
| "CERT-recognised" / "Category-1 registered" | Nothing. **xLoop is not on the PKCERT register in any category** |
| "NEPRA-approved auditor" | "We audit against the NEPRA IT/OT Regulations 2022" |
| Any implied regulatory endorsement | — |

## 2. Third-party outcomes presented as xLoop's

**The highest-risk category.** Presenting a partner's customer results as your own is easy to
disprove and unrecoverable when it is.

| ❌ Never | Why |
|---|---|
| BP Retail, Morgan Sindall, Electronic Arts as xLoop case studies | These are **Voltaware's** customers. They appear under "Impact We've Created" in xLoop's internal xVoltIQ document |
| "15% reduction in residential energy waste" / "20% reduction in peak load" as xLoop outcomes | Currently live on `/xlab/xVoltIQ`. **Almost certainly partner outcomes.** Remove or attribute |
| Any Graymatics, Adosterr, Digitrack or Vortex AI outcome as xLoop's | Ownership unresolved — **verify before any use** |
| Partner technology described without attribution | If xLoop resells or white-labels it, say so |

**Rule: if xLoop did not do the work, xLoop does not claim the outcome — even when the technology is licensed.**

## 3. Unsourced statistics

Prohibited until sourced to a client, a period and a method:

99.7% detection rate · $50M recalls prevented · 92% client retention · 98% customer satisfaction ·
40% increased visitor engagement · 60% higher query volume · 45% faster order processing ·
78% decrease in order errors · 30% operational efficiency · 25% wait-time reduction ·
40% reduction in security incidents · 97% diagnostic accuracy · 2M+ charts generated ·
$25M downtime prevented · 300+ assets monitored · 75% faster retrieval · 94% user satisfaction

**Note the internal contradiction:** the first two and the last three in the xVision group
describe the *same product* with *mutually exclusive* numbers. Publishing either while both are in
circulation invites the question xLoop cannot currently answer.

## 4. Contested corporate facts

Do not publish until adjudicated: founding year · headcount · number of countries ·
number of solutions delivered · number of clients · diversity percentages.

**Interim safe wording:** *"Offices in Karachi, San Mateo, Dubai and Doha, serving clients across
Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Europe and North America."* True under every version in circulation.

## 5. Client identity

| ❌ Never | Why |
|---|---|
| Naming **ACT Wind** | Anonymised reference only |
| Naming **Thames Water** or any client from `Projects xLoop.xlsx` not already public | Internal register. Public naming requires permission |
| Naming any NEPRA target company as a prospect or client | The 87-row register is restricted personal and commercial data |
| Naming **Meezan**, or any client appearing only in a demo video | No permission evidence found |
| Naming Majid Al Futtaim, Standard Bank, ABSA, Lombard Insurance, SS&C, WeFi Tech | Internal pipeline entries, not confirmed clients |

**Approved to name:** only clients with a live portfolio page, a live logo placement, or a signed
testimonial — Alfalah Investments, Beythak, Eatsy, HBL Asset Management, Serefin, Skillforte,
FitLynk, Cloud Titans, plus the current logo wall.

## 6. Confidential commercial information

Never publish: pricing (including the PKR 350,000 NEPRA pre-audit figure) · TAM/SAM/SOM analysis ·
ARR projections · the xCrowdIQ Qatar plan, its named target ministries or named target venues ·
funding requirements and utilisation from the board deck · gross margin, EBITDA, deal-closure
ratios · named prospects in any pipeline document · the NEPRA target register.

## 7. Fear-selling and superlatives

Prohibited by xLoop's own brand guidelines, which list this register as **"not like this"**:

❌ "Utilize our superior tech to avoid falling behind."
❌ "We have the tech know-how you're obviously missing."
❌ "The leading AI firm in..." — unsubstantiated superlative.
❌ "World-class", "best-in-class", "industry-leading" without a citation.
❌ Invented threat statistics. Particularly with the NEPRA audience, who would catch it.

**Note:** the market-intelligence figures in the AI Security CMS spec (73% of enterprises suffered
an AI-related breach; $4.8M average cost; WEF $5.7 trillion by 2030) are **external claims with no
source recorded in the corpus**. They may well be real, but they must be traced to a named
Level 2/3 publication and cited before use. Uncited, they fall under this section.

## 8. Anything that would need retracting

The test: *if a client's compliance officer, a regulator, or a journalist read this and asked for
the evidence, could xLoop produce it within an hour?* If not, do not publish it.
