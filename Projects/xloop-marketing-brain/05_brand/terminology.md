# Terminology

One word per thing. This file exists because the corpus currently uses several.

## 1. Company name

`VERIFIED INTERNAL FACT` — from the official brand guidelines:

- **"xLoop Digital"** is the official company name. **"xLoop"** is used in marketing communication.
- **Always small `x`, capital `L`.** "exemplifying the synergy of expertise and innovation."
- Never abbreviate or alter in public communication.

❌ XLoop · Xloop · XLOOP · X-Loop · xloop (lowercase L) · xLoop Digital Services (except in the legal entity context, where the ACT Wind proposal uses it)

⚠️ **Live inconsistency:** the ACT Wind proposal uses "XLoop", "Xloop" and "xLoop" within the same
document, and the NEPRA compliance letter filename is `XLoop_NEPRA_Compliance_Letter.docx`.

## 3. Products — ⚠️ naming needs a decision

| Current usage | Problem |
|---|---|
| **App Pilot** | Used for the asset-management LLM investment assistant |
| **HR App Pilot** | Used for the HR/Odoo bot |
| `/xlab/app-pilot` **and** `/xlab/hr-app-pilot` | Both exist in the sitemap; only HR App Pilot is in the nav |

`Marketing Brand Issues.docx` raises this itself: *"Why is App Pilot not okay, but HR App Pilot is
okay? Is the tech significantly different?"*

**They are different products.** One is an investor-facing document assistant; the other is an
HR workflow bot integrated with Odoo. **Recommendation: give them distinct names.** Two products
sharing a name confuses buyers, splits search authority, and makes case studies ambiguous.

| Product | Correct form |
|---|---|
| **xVision** | Not XVision, x-vision, X Vision |
| **xServe** | Not xserve, XServe |
| **xCrowdIQ** | Not xCrowdIq, XCrowdIQ, CrowdIQ. ⚠️ `Market Considerations` uses "XCrowdIQ" |
| **xVoltIQ** | Not xVoltaware. ⚠️ Internal docs still use "xVoltaware" and "VOLTAWARE" |
| **Chat Genie** | Two words. Not ChatGenie, Chatgenie |
| **Meta Human** | ⚠️ Corpus uses "Meta Human", "MetaHuman" and "xLoop MetaHuman" |
| **Design Studio** | Part of xLab |

## 4. Service naming — recommended shifts

| ❌ Technology-named (current) | ✅ Outcome-named (recommended) |
|---|---|
| Large Language Models | Document and knowledge intelligence |
| Generative AI | Content and process automation |
| AI Agents & Autonomous Systems | Autonomous workflows |
| Computer Vision | Visual operations intelligence |
| Predictive Analytics | Forecasting and demand intelligence |
| Machine Learning | Custom models |
| Staff / resource augmentation | **Specialist engineering capacity** |

Keep the technology terms in body copy and metadata for search. Change the *page titles*, which
is what buyers and AI answer engines read as the offer.

## 8. Words to standardise

| Prefer | Over |
|---|---|
| clients | customers (for services); *customers* is fine for xLab products |
| engagement | project (for consulting work) |
| assessment | audit — **unless it genuinely is an audit against a standard** |
| specialists / engineers | resources |
| build | develop, create, craft |

**The assessment/audit distinction is commercially important**, especially in the NEPRA context
where "audit" carries a regulatory meaning. Use it precisely.

## 9. Spelling convention
