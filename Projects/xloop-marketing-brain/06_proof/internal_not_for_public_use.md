# INTERNAL — NOT FOR PUBLIC USE

**Nothing in this file may appear in any public marketing material, website page, social post,
proposal to a third party, or AI-generated content intended for publication.**

It is recorded here because Release 1 was asked to identify confidential and restricted material
rather than ignore it — and because a future workflow must be able to recognise what it must not
publish. Its existence in the Marketing Brain is precisely so it can be excluded.

## Category A — Named clients not approved for public use

`Projects xLoop.xlsx` is an internal register of 31 engagements with **named clients**. Several of
these clients appear publicly only in anonymised case studies, and at least one appears in the
public material as a generic descriptor.

**Handling rules:**
- ❌ Never publish a client name from this register unless it independently appears on the live logo wall, a live portfolio page, or a signed testimonial.
- ❌ Never publish a detail that makes an anonymised client identifiable by elimination — e.g. pairing "water utility company" with a country and a technology stack.
- ❌ Never publish pipeline entries as if they were clients. Several rows are opportunities with a named internal owner, not delivered work.
- ✅ The anonymised descriptors already in the public proof library are safe.

**Specific restrictions:**
- The water utility engagement — **the internal register names the client; public material must not.**
- The NEPRA audit client — **ACT Wind. Anonymised as "a 30 MW wind IPP" only.**
- The Meezan demo material — no permission evidence found.
- Rows with an internal owner name and no delivery detail are prospects, not clients.
