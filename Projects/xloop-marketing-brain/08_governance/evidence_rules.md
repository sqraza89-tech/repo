# Evidence Rules

Binding on every future workflow, human or AI, that produces xLoop material.

---

## 1. The source hierarchy

| Level | What | Examples | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 — Internal primary** | Approved xLoop documents and systems | Live website; official brand guidelines; approved case studies; signed proposals; verified project records; client-approved material | The **only** valid evidence for a claim about xLoop |
| **L2 — External primary** | Authoritative originals | Regulators (NEPRA, PKCERT), government sources, official filings, standards bodies (ISO, IEC, NIST), official company announcements | Market facts, regulatory facts |
| **L3 — High-quality secondary** | Recognised research | McKinsey, Gartner, Forrester, Deloitte, major financial publications, respected industry press | Market context. **Always cited by name and date** |
| **L4 — General** | Everything else | Blogs, vendor content, aggregators, social posts | Only where appropriate, always labelled |

**Absolute rule:**
> **Never treat an external source as evidence of an xLoop capability.**
> A Gartner report on AI security market growth is evidence about the market. It is not evidence
> that xLoop can deliver AI security.

## 2. Evidence classes

Every significant statement about xLoop carries one:

| Class | Meaning | Publishable |
|---|---|---|
| **VERIFIED INTERNAL FACT** | Confirmed in an L1 source | ✅ Yes |
| **VERIFIED EXTERNAL FACT** | Confirmed in an L2 source, cited | ✅ Yes, with citation |
| **SUPPORTED INFERENCE** | A reasonable conclusion from evidence, but not stated anywhere | ⚠️ Internally yes; publicly only as opinion, clearly framed |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Asserted somewhere, not confirmed | ❌ No |
| **EVIDENCE REQUIRED** | Needed for the argument, not found | ❌ No — and flag it |
| **OUTDATED — VERIFY** | Was true, may not be | ❌ No until re-verified |

## 3. Never fabricate

Under no circumstances invent: customers · outcomes · statistics · certifications · partnerships ·
implementations · capabilities · revenue · performance figures · market share · client names ·
case-study results · quotes · testimonials · awards · analyst mentions.

**If the evidence is unavailable, write `EVIDENCE REQUIRED` and say what would resolve it.**
An honest gap is a work item. A plausible invention is a liability that surfaces in a sales meeting.

## 4. The statistic rule

**No number may be published without all three of:**
1. **Source** — the client, project or publication it comes from.
2. **Period** — when it was measured.
3. **Method** — how it was measured, at least in outline.

`VERIFIED INTERNAL FACT` — at least eleven statistics currently published on xloopdigital.com have
none of the three. Two of them describe the same product with mutually exclusive values.

**How the drift happened, and how to stop it:** `Homepage 2.0.docx` contains statistics for
products that no longer exist (`PredictaMax`, `Healthcare AI`). `SUPPORTED INFERENCE` — figures
were generated to fill design layouts rather than sourced from delivery. **The rule that prevents
recurrence: design layouts must accept a blank where a number is not available.**

## 5. Third-party evidence

**If xLoop did not do the work, xLoop does not claim the outcome — even when the technology is licensed.**

Before publishing any product outcome, answer:
1. Did xLoop build this, or license/resell it?
2. Did xLoop deliver the engagement that produced the outcome?
3. Is the outcome the client's, the partner's, or xLoop's?
4. Has the partner approved xLoop referencing it?

Open cases: **xVoltIQ/xVoltaware** (BP Retail, Morgan Sindall, Electronic Arts appear in the
internal document; outcome figures are live on the site), **Graymatics**, **Adosterr**,
**Digitrack**, **Vortex AI**, **MAK Knowledge Desk**.

## 6. Client confidentiality

- A client may be named publicly only if they appear on the live logo wall, a live portfolio page, or a signed testimonial.
- Anonymisation must actually anonymise. "A water utility company" is safe; "a 30 MW wind IPP in Jhimpir with two plant sites" may not be.
- The internal project register and the NEPRA target register are **restricted** and never a source for public claims.
- When in doubt, ask the account owner. Never infer permission from the fact that a document exists.

## 7. Recency

| Age | Treatment |
|---|---|
| < 6 months | Current |
| 6–12 months | Verify before use |
| 12–24 months | **OUTDATED — VERIFY** |
| > 24 months | Do not use without re-verification |

Applied examples: the GA4 top-pages export (Aug 2024–May 2025, previous site structure) is
historic only; `Home page Content.docx` (Dec 2024) is superseded; `Mega menu.docx` describes a
navigation that no longer exists.

## 8. Conflict resolution

When two L1 sources disagree:
1. **Do not pick one silently.** Record both.
2. Prefer the **live website** for what is publicly claimed.
3. Prefer the **most recent** document for direction of travel — but note that recency is not accuracy (the newest deck claims *fewer* solutions delivered than the older one).
4. **Escalate to a human.** All twelve current conflicts are listed in `01_positioning/positioning_conflicts.md` §F.
5. **Publish nothing on the contested point** until it is resolved.

## 9. Evidence in AI-generated content

Any workflow producing xLoop material must:
1. Cite the source for every factual claim, at the sentence level, in a draft.
2. Refuse to produce a statistic not in `05_brand/approved_claims.md`.
3. Flag every `SUPPORTED INFERENCE` explicitly rather than presenting it as fact.
4. Never fill a gap with a plausible number.
5. Check every claim against `05_brand/prohibited_claims.md` before output.
6. Surface uncertainty in the output, not only in the reasoning.
