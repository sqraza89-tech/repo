# Search Demand

## 1. The methodological warning that governs this entire file

`VERIFIED INTERNAL FACT` — **xLoop has no keyword volume tool.** Available tools are GA4, Google
Search Console and Screaming Frog (free). Every "search demand" figure in the corpus comes from
**Google Trends relative interest**, which is not volume and cannot be compared between terms.

**Nothing in this file should be read as a volume estimate.** Where the corpus implies volume,
that implication is flagged.

**A worked example of why this matters.** `Search Trends & Ads Research.docx` lists top interest
regions for many terms. **St. Helena** — population roughly 4,500 — appears as a top region for
"AI Consulting Services", "Management Consulting Firms", "Best Consulting Firms", "AI for business
automation", "AI for predictive analytics", "AI chatbot development", "Digital Engineering",
"Custom Software Dev" and "AI for Customer Service".

That is not a market signal. It is the standard small-denominator artefact in Trends normalisation.
The same artefact very likely explains why **Kenya** and **Rwanda** appear prominently — and those
two have been carried into the brief as priority geographies. See `geographic_strategy.md` §2.

**Two findings:**

1. **Brand demand is captured well.** Position 1.16, 41.8% CTR. Nothing is broken about how xLoop ranks for its own name — though `x loop` at position 6.02 suggests some brand SERP work is available.
2. **Commercial demand is not captured at all.** Zero clicks on every non-brand query.

### The three non-brand query clusters, and what each tells us

**Cluster A — research questions the blog ranks for (0 clicks)**
`agent hospital china` (43 impressions, pos. 6.6) · `big data analytics for telecom` (33, pos. 14.1) ·
`ai enterprise solutions failure` (18, **pos. 1.0**) · `customer onboarding in retail banking` (17) ·
`what data related reason is a common reason for ai projects failure` (pos. 1.0) ·
`what is a major challenge faced by ai proof of concepts` (pos. 1.0) ·
`cio decision framework e-clinical vendor selection ai capabilities` (multiple variants, pos. 2.5–4.5)

**xLoop ranks position 1 for several of these and earns no clicks.** These are questions people ask,
not problems people buy solutions for. The content is genuinely good; it is aimed at the wrong
moment in the buying process. This is the Q3 review's *"content geared towards education and
awareness"* finding, visible in the data.

**Cluster B — brand confusion**
`xloop loan app` (15) · `xloop loans` (9) · `x loop loan app` (2) · `xtoop`, `xlooo`, `xlotp technology`

A different entity. Real users, wrong company. Worth monitoring; not worth chasing.

**Cluster C — weak commercial signals, poor positions**
`visual intelligence technology` (12, pos. 55.6) · `ai visual intelligence` (8, pos. 73.5) ·
`visual intelligence ai` (9, pos. 86.1) · `enterprise data transformation` (2, pos. 18) ·
`ai predictive analytics in healthcare` (5, pos. 90.4) · `software house near me` (3, **pos. 1.0**)

**"Visual intelligence" is the most interesting entry here.** Three variants, 29 combined
impressions, positions 55–86. That is a term with commercial relevance to xVision where xLoop
appears but ranks nowhere. It is a real, addressable gap.

## 3. Query clusters evaluated

Assessed on relevance, intent and commercial potential rather than volume, because volume is unknown.

### Cluster 1 — AI Security & Governance ⭐ **highest priority**
`ai security assessment` · `ai red teaming` · `ai security audit` · `llm security testing` ·
`ai governance consulting` · `ai risk assessment` · `shadow ai` · `llm vulnerabilities` ·
`ai model security` · `agent security` · `multi agent security` · `autonomous agent risk`

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Intent | **High** — assessment/audit/consulting are procurement verbs |
| xLoop relevance | **Very high** — real service, real findings, real credential |
| Competition | **Low** — few firms have claimed this category |
| Commercial potential | **High** — $25K–200K, 2–6 month cycle |
| AI-search potential | **Very high** — emerging topics with no settled answers |

xLoop's own ICP research says it directly: *"This should become an ownership category."* Agreed.

### Cluster 2 — NEPRA compliance ⭐ **highest priority**
`nepra compliance audit` · `nepra it ot regulations` · `nepra security regulations 2022` ·
`scada security audit pakistan` · `ot penetration testing pakistan` · `nepra compliance services`

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Intent | **Highest of any cluster** — regulatory search is buying search |
| Volume | Low absolute, and **that is fine** — 87 companies is the entire market |
| Competition | Real (SPS, Catalyic, Ebryx, INTECH) but **shallow on content** |
| Content moat | **None of them publish the clause-level requirement**; the leader's page has no FAQ |
| AI-search potential | **Very high** — an unanswered regulatory question |

**Adding a Regulation 12 / PowerCERT answer would be an entirely unclaimed position.**

### Cluster 3 — Data modernisation
`data modernization consulting` · `data engineering services` · `data platform consulting` ·
`microsoft fabric consulting` · `data governance consulting` · `ai ready data architecture` ·
`data quality framework`

High intent, strong xLoop proof, moderate competition. ⚠️ **Microsoft Fabric appears in xLoop's own
ICP research as required buyer evidence and appears nowhere else in the corpus** — no partner
listing, no service page, no blog. Either a capability gap or a marketing gap; worth establishing which.

### Cluster 4 — Enterprise AI consulting
`enterprise ai consulting` · `ai strategy consulting` · `ai transformation consulting` ·
`ai advisory services` · `enterprise ai roadmap` · `ai implementation consulting`

Biggest apparent demand, highest competition, weakest xLoop differentiation and thinnest proof.
The corpus notes interest "started picking up in May '25 and peaked in mid-August", concentrated in
USA, UAE, India, Canada, Singapore.

**Recommendation: compete here on long-tail and industry-specific variants** (`ai consulting for
banks`, `ai implementation energy sector`) **rather than on the head terms**, until proof exists.

### Cluster 5 — Visual intelligence / computer vision
`visual intelligence ai` · `computer vision quality control` · `ai video analytics retail` ·
`branch analytics ai`

**xLoop already appears for three "visual intelligence" variants at positions 55–86.** Real
relevance, no ranking. A defined, addressable opportunity for xVision — blocked mainly by the
conflicting statistics problem, not by search.

### Cluster 6 — Engineering capacity
`ai engineering services` · `dedicated development team` · `engineering staff augmentation` ·
`ai software development company`

High volume, high competition, price-led, and — per `buyer_personas.md` §5 — **this buyer arrives
by referral, not by search.** Low priority for content investment despite being commercial priority #1.

## 5. What must happen before any of this works

Two prerequisites, in order:

1. **Fix the duplicate titles, duplicate metas and missing canonicals.** With 32 pages sharing one title, no keyword strategy can succeed. See `website_search_ai_discovery.md` §2.
2. **Get a keyword volume tool.** Every prioritisation above is reasoned, not measured. Ahrefs or Semrush would convert this file from judgement into evidence — and it is the single most useful marketing purchase available.

## 6. Paid search

`SUPPORTED INFERENCE` — the corpus recommends LinkedIn only for paid, at ~$5k/quarter, with a
40% USA / 30% Qatar-ME / 30% Pakistan split. That is reasonable for B2B at this budget.

**One challenge:** with the site's current conversion architecture — no diagnostic tool, unsourced
proof, duplicate metadata — paid traffic would land on pages that cannot convert it.
**Recommendation: fix conversion before buying traffic.** The organic and technical work has a
higher return at this budget level.
