## Visual identity
- **Feel (Sana, 23 Oct 2026):** chic, vibrant, rich, elegant, aspirational. Moms should want to *be* this. v1 (rounded sans, park greens, cartoon heart/hills) was rejected as "makes me feel old"
- **v3 direction (current, 23 Oct 2026):** cool + chic, lululemon-ad vibe, body-positive ("comfortable in your body and getting into shape"). Abstract figure from bold ribbon/brush strokes with circle head + heavy wide geometric sans. Colours: electric orange #FF4D12, hot fuchsia #E6198A (gradient OK), plum #3B0A2E, cream #FFF4EA. Logo being explored in ChatGPT from `Notes/2026-10-23-moms-on-the-move-logo-gpt-prompt.md`. Sana disliked the GPT output (chunky "X" legs, floating head, bubble font). Redrawn as vector in `Notes/2026-10-23-moms-on-the-move-logo-v3.html`: two woven tapered ribbons (S-curve body + arm-to-leg sweep) + circle head; wordmark Archivo Expanded ExtraBold, aubergine #2A0A22; palette orange #FF4D12, hot coral #F2265A, fuchsia #E0198A, cream #FFF6EE. Then Sana shared monoline references (arc figures, circle heads, M-wave lettermarks, linked-arm group, curvy line-art woman) → v4 `Notes/2026-10-23-moms-on-the-move-logo-v4.html`: 4 original monoline marks: 1 Arc Stride, 2 Rising M (recommended), 3 Together, 4 Free Spirit. Same palette + Archivo Expanded wordmark. References are existing logos, so use the approach only, never copy. Sana: v4 lines had constant weight, no balance/symmetry/hierarchy; liked "Together" best → **v5 `Notes/2026-10-23-moms-on-the-move-logo-v5.html`** (current lead): three crescent bodies built on circles (thick shoulders, hairline hand tips), mirrored symmetry, larger/taller centre figure, centre arms over the side figures; stacked centred lockup "moms" (Archivo Expanded 800) + "ON THE MOVE" (tracked 500). Sana approved v5 as "one version"
- **v6 (26 Oct 2026), muted/elegant second version:** `Notes/2026-10-26-moms-on-the-move-logo-v6.html`. Sana: "not feeling the gradient", wants muted, elegant, decent. Two moms running hand in hand (one wave of linked arms + two forward-leaning leg arches that echo "m"), brush-tapered strokes. Palette: Terracotta #C2694E, Dusty Mauve #A35D76, Blush #E9D3CB, Bone #F6F0E9, Sage #9BA78F, Plum Ink #3B2433, Light Mauve #C99AA9. Wordmark Lexend Exa Medium "moms" + Light tracked "ON THE MOVE"
- **v6 colour refresh (26 Oct 2026):** Sana found terracotta/mauve "too dull, looks old" → 3 fresher palettes on the same mark in `Notes/2026-10-26-moms-on-the-move-logo-v6-palettes.html`: 1 Peach #F5967A + Rose #E0739A (ink Berry #45213A), 2 Peach + Fresh Sage #7DB894 (ink Forest #22372D), 3 Peach + Lilac #9D8FE0 (ink Navy #272750). Sana liked all three → **colour system**: Peach is the constant; partner colour per activity: Fresh Sage = walks/jogs (and main logo, Peach + Sage on Cream), Lilac = park pilates, Rose = brunch club. Washes: Sage #E3F1E7, Lilac #ECE8FB, Petal #FCE4DC; Cream #FFF9F5; one shared Ink #2B2540
